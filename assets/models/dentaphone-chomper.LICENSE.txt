Morphazoid modifications:

  - separated all 32 native tooth shells for one-to-one musical interaction;
  - classified and named 16 upper and 16 lower teeth;
  - split the connected mouth shell into independently hinged gum sections;
  - centered, display-scaled, smoothed, and organized the mesh beneath named
    upper and lower jaw nodes;
  - converted the result from Wavefront OBJ to glTF Binary (GLB).

The reproducible conversion script is scripts/build-dentaphone-chomper.py.
