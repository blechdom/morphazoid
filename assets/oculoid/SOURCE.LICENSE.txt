asset.extras. The public listing was marked downloadable and CC Attribution.
The bundled 2,728,632-byte Objaverse GLB retains its two separate, textured
globe and iris meshes; it contains no skin, eyelids, animation or eyelashes.
The published Sketchfab export and this mirrored GLB have different byte
counts; do not describe them as byte-for-byte identical distributions.

Morphazoid modifications happen at runtime, not in eye.glb: the globe is
normalized and turned to face the camera; original textures remain
available. New authored geometry supplies two deformable eyelid patches
joined to a posterior skin hemisphere with selectable chrome and matte-brown
finishes, optional patterns, black inward surfaces, and asymmetric, separately
movable, rounded margins. Globe and shell are spherical in all three axes.
Iris palettes and a custom color preserve the texture's fibers; independent
eyeball colors tint its separate globe mesh without changing the downloaded asset.
A single teardrop grows
from the inner lid corner and falls vertically without a skin streak;
there is no separate tear duct or caruncle;
outward-swept eyelashes, fine scleral vessels, a pupil overlay and
lighting complete the eye. The globe can rotate past normal human gaze
limits behind the lids, or the entire assembly can turn together up to
90 degrees from front on either axis. These
additions, their shapes and their sound mapping are artistic
approximations, not an anatomy teaching model or a validated ocular simulation.
Attribute DEGUIDER, link the source, keep this CC BY notice and describe
changes when reusing the model. The creator does not endorse Morphazoid.

The 19,760-byte bundled derivative retains the one static teardrop's 526
vertex positions, normals and 960 triangles. It removes textures, material,
UVs, tangents and source scene unit-conversion transforms (the original GLB
is 2,602,432 bytes). It has no rig or animation. To reproduce the extraction
with the downloaded, hash-matching original:
  python3 scripts/prepare-oculoid-drop.py \
    /path/to/cc0_-_drops.glb assets/oculoid/drop.glb
At runtime Morphazoid applies its own translucent water material to a copy,
turns the tip up and leans the bulb away from the eyelid shell while keeping
the pointed tip at the joined lid corner. It grows there, then drops straight
down without a skin streak. This is an artistic animation,
not a fluid simulation or an endorsement by plaggy. Preserve the CC BY 4.0
attribution and the modification notice when sharing this geometry.

The public Sketchfab listing marked the model downloadable and CC Attribution;
the GLB embeds the author, CC-BY-4.0 license, source and title in asset.extras.
The 1,681,860-byte Objaverse GLB is bundled unchanged, with five material
groups and full 3D frames, lenses, bridge and temples. Its embedded FBX
conversion already presents the glasses upright. Morphazoid scales it at
runtime to fit the spherical eyelid, adds a small visible brow bridge and
shortens its depth so the temples do not pierce the skin, and darkens the
lenses for readability.
It moves as one connected pair with the upper lid (or perches on the exposed
globe when lids are hidden). Procedural glasses are a fallback if this optional
file fails to load. Credit Kimppo, link the original work and license, and
describe these runtime changes when sharing it. Kimppo does not endorse Oculoid.
