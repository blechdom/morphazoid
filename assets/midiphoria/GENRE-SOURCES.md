# Midiphoria genre MIDI sources

Retrieved 2026-10-07. These are original compositions posted by their creators, not transcriptions of commercial hit songs. All three have explicit CC0 licensing on the linked OpenGameArt submission pages. Original MIDI bytes are unchanged; filenames are shortened.

Rock Theme Four is creator-tagged Rock. Rewind is creator-tagged Jazz and chiptune. Disco Challenge comes from Komiku’s explicitly described fantasy-disco soundtrack; the creator lists all MIDI files as their own original album compositions. The local genre labels support browsing and do not claim that these are well-known pop songs.

Credit strings, source URLs, exact download URLs, archive members, licenses and SHA-256 hashes are in `collection.json`. The CC0 dedication is linked at https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ and its complete text is bundled as `licenses/CC0-1.0.txt`, from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/spdx/license-list-data/main/text/CC0-1.0.txt.

Each file passed MThd validation and complete parsing by SpessaSynth Core 4.3.22. Rock Theme Four has five tracks and 1,313 events; Rewind has four tracks and 4,234 events; Disco Challenge has eleven tracks and 4,464 events. Durations were calculated by the parser using each file’s tempo map.

The source pages and exact download URLs recorded in `collection.json` identify the original downloads. Only the three curated MIDI files are shipped from those sources.

These creator tracks appear alongside clearly labelled original genre demos and the local MIDI-file chooser. They are not presented as commercial chart hits.
