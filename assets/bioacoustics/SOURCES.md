menus in Crickets, Strophe Lab, and Acoustic Manifold. Morphazoid does not imply
that a recordist, source archive, government agency, or Creative Commons licensor
endorses this project.

The bundled media bytes are unchanged from the cited downloads. During
listening and analysis, the browser may resample compressed audio, and
Morphazoid selects one channel. The dolphin WAV is parsed locally at its source
sample rate. Route auditions extract detected event slices and add short edge
fades. Generated model audio is computed separately and never incorporates
source samples.

## Human and Indigenous recordings

No Indigenous or other human song recording is bundled here. Public access or
copyright status alone does not establish consent for computational extraction,
rearrangement, training, or resynthesis. Acoustic Manifold instead links to
community-governed and institutional archives with their access conditions,
alongside the CARE Principles, Local Contexts Traditional Knowledge Labels, and
the Protocols for Native American Archival Materials. Any future inclusion must
record the named cultural authority, community protocol, allowed audience and
uses, provenance, right of review or withdrawal, and explicit permission for
transformative or generative use.
