A chiptune instrument with five pitched voices, drums, a noise sweep, and stereo echo. Select a performer to edit its sound and sequence.

Seven performers dance to the notes and rhythms of their own sounding parts. Select a character to open its editor. Use Controls to replace any dancer with its sound knobs. Mix and step controls remain available in either view. Character skins change appearance without changing sound.

From left to right: Drums, Bass, Arp, Lead, Upper A, Upper B, and Noise.

Click a character to select it; drag horizontally and vertically to adjust its effects. Arrow keys adjust effects; Page Up and Page Down select performers; Home restores the neutral position. M toggles mute; S toggles solo. Muting wins over solo.

In Pattern pad, horizontal movement changes pattern seed and vertical movement changes pitch range.

Drag notes for pitch and the lower bars for volume. Double-click, Enter, or Space toggles a step. Follow tracks the sounding step; Each character’s Steps control chooses 1 to 32 active steps. Zoom and X pan control the visible steps. Song arrangement follows the original composition; Independent loops repeats editable patterns and lets you choose a song loop. Arrow keys select or change notes, Page Up and Page Down switch lanes, A resets a step, N enables it, and R silences it. Changes keep playback running.
