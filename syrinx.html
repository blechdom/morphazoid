The call is a breakpoint gesture over physical controls. Change any baseline while it plays; the trajectory continues around your edited anatomy.

Choose an animal and call, turn on audio, then press Play call. Hold the Breath button for manual pressure. On the canvas, drag the source handle vertically for tension, the cavity handle for coupling, the mouth handle vertically for opening, or the tract handle horizontally for physical length. Space starts or stops the selected call when focus is outside a control. Press Escape to stop the current call and release pressure.
