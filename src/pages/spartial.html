About the controls

The fingerprint contains 1–32 sine partials. Frequency spacing at 1 and inharmonicity at 0 produce the harmonic series. Lower spacing compresses their pitches; higher spacing expands them. Inharmonicity offsets upper partials while preserving the fundamental.

The diagram shows speaker routing order. Colored dots are individual partial destinations; faint dots are silent. In One speaker mode all partials share the highlighted output. Click or drag to a speaker to choose it.

Rotation Play/Pause controls movement only. Hold sustains the selected note or chord independently. Rotation follows the tempo; pausing retains the angle. Eight voices can sound at once. Cascade edits affect the next note; spectrum and space edits affect held notes.

Delay is measured in milliseconds per partial. At Even spacing and 0% variation every gap is equal. Silent and out-of-range partials still occupy a step; long fade-ins can blur the entrances. Lower Fade in for sharper attacks. The timing strip shows scheduled entrances, not volume. Restart briefly fades the held note or chord before starting its cascade again.

Layouts come from Surround for Safety. LFE stays silent. Stereo previews left/right only; physical surround needs a multichannel output and matching numbered interface patch.
