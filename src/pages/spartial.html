About the controls

The fingerprint has one bank of bars. Custom partials maps them to individual sine partials; Tonewheel and Pipe organ map them to nine footage registers. In the organ mappings, 8′ follows the played pitch and 16′ is one octave below. Frequency spacing at 1 and inharmonicity at 0 produce the harmonic series. Lower spacing compresses their pitches; higher spacing expands them. Inharmonicity offsets upper partials while preserving the fundamental.

The diagram shows speaker routing order. Colored dots show partial routing before rotary; faint dots are silent. H and D follow the rotary horn and drum. In One speaker mode all partials share the highlighted output. Click or drag to a speaker to choose it.

Rotation Play/Pause controls movement only. Hold sustains the selected note or chord independently. Rotation follows the tempo; pausing retains the angle. Eight voices can sound at once. Cascade edits affect the next note; spectrum and space edits affect held notes.

Delay is measured in milliseconds per partial. At Even spacing and 0% variation every gap is equal. Silent and out-of-range partials still occupy a step; long fade-ins can blur the entrances. Lower Fade in for sharper attacks. The timing strip shows scheduled entrances, not volume. Restart briefly fades the held note or chord before starting its cascade again.

The granular layer captures the additive sound before the mixer and master volume. Files are mixed to mono before each grain is placed in surround. C4 plays a file at its original pitch; the latest note transposes its grains. Freeze holds the live source, while Rotation Pause holds spatial movement.

Organ synthesis and the Leslie-inspired effect are documented in the organ research notes. These are original additive approximations, with no sampled organ recordings.

Layouts come from Surround for Safety. Omit LFE channel keeps that output silent without renumbering the other channels. Uncheck it to include LFE in partial and grain routing; no subwoofer filter is applied. Stereo previews left/right only; physical surround needs a multichannel output and matching numbered interface patch.
