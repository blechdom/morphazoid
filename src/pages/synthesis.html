Notes before sound; effects after sound

The arpeggiator creates timed note events. The tuning map turns their pitch relationships into frequencies. The synthesizer turns those events into audio; the processor transforms that audio.

Mic, files, loops and test signals replace the synthesizer. They do not pass through its arpeggiator or tuning map. A processor's own carrier frequency or resonance is a separate control, not an automatic retuning of recorded audio.

Speech synthesis and Singing synthesis use Voicesaurus’s native engines. Speech has its own text or phonetic controls; singing has an editable score. They replace the regular synthesizer, arpeggiator and tuning controls, with optional processing afterward.

Choose a local audio file; it stays in your browser. Sample loops include licensed recordings and original rendered demos—not a commercial synthesizer's factory library.
