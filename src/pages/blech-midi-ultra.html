About & controls

A centered Math workbox appears on load. Free Math drops the current formula once without a frame; its corner arrow opens the formula popup with a Workbox action. The Math workbox's picture menu chooses a formula, and its Math-controls icon opens the equation and graph knobs beside the frame. Changing the equation keeps the frame where you placed it. Drag the silent frame for pitch and delay, its grips to resize, or its rotate handle to turn it. The ch1 button shows the next layer's MIDI channel and opens a nearby channel knob and instrument menu; the inline volume knob shows 0–127 for the next layer's gain (not note velocity). Drag vertically or use arrow keys. One beat is one quarter note at the master tempo. Audio is armed separately.

The workbox actions are Drop ↓ (start with an editable frame), ↻ (loop), the green disk/check (drop straight to Layers), channel ▾, volume, Reset (restore frame, sound, Loop and Math notes; clear a handmade box's additions), and × (discard the draft). Clicking away tucks an unfinished draft; the square icon restores it. On a playing layer, ✓ closes its frame and × removes and stops only that layer. Mute and Solo keep its timing running; each strip has volume, channel and pan. Turning a layer's Loop off finishes the current pass. Master Play/Pause pauses or resumes without deleting; Trash removes all notes, layers and drafts. Four audio clips play at once; a new sound may replace the oldest unpinned clip. Top-rail squares stay until removed, but neither rails nor Free Draw create Layers.

Select a layer's name or activity map to show its floating rectangle. Drag, resize or rotate it to change the running MIDI while retaining its playback position. Its gear opens a local popup for channel, pitch, delay, width, height, rotation and instrument. Close it with ×, Escape or a click outside; hovering does nothing. Selecting another layer switches the rectangle; choosing a generator icon returns to a new drop.

Free Draw sounds once as you draw, without a workbox or automatic loop. Its caret opens Pencil, crosshair Line, Rectangle, Ellipse and Triangle. With Line, click anchors to connect them, then double-click or press Space to drop the finished path; Escape cancels. Drag and release Rectangle, Ellipse or Triangle to drop a shape. The Size slider (1–8) grows the brush in pitch and time together; its preview shows the Square, Circle or sparse Spray nib. Shapes can be Outline or Solid. Repeat and the double-click latch apply only to live Pencil. MIDI notes fall and sound, but Free Draw leaves no permanent sketch. Free Stamp has its own one-shot icon; its bottom caret opens a thumbnail picture menu and independent width/height controls. Free Text places a blinking cursor and a nearby font, size, Bold, Italic and horizontal/vertical toolbar. Type silent letters and press Return or tap another spot to drop once and start a new word; Escape cancels. Its corner arrow opens the advanced Text workbox. For a loop, open Workbox and use the Draw, Stamp, Text or fourth Math icon beside its top-right corner. Click an active icon again to move the frame. Choose a Math picture, then tap inside the same silent workbox to add it; no second box appears. Type a message once, then click to place silent text as often as you like. Loop Stamp's bottom caret opens only its picture picker and a size knob; 1× restores the original stamp proportions. Stamp placements remain silent until Drop, with Loop on by default. The Workbox Draw caret shares the Line and shape gestures; Add + opens prepared notes and More sources for the optional grid, images, scripts and sequences. Eraser touches only the selected layer.

Double-click with Pencil to latch a Held note without repeated attacks. Master Pause releases it; Resume rejoins it. Free Draw notes have no layer; Trash clears them with everything else.

The grey rail directly below the tools makes notes rain on one pitch: tap to repeat quickly, hold for slower pulses, or double-tap to sustain. Tap its little square again to stop and remove that note. The top of the rail picks black keys, the bottom white keys. The grey Comb bar at the left makes a whole horizontal row: tap anywhere to play every pitch once; hold to preview 1 on / 1 off, 2 on / 2 off, and larger pitch blocks; or drag up to delay. Release to play the row once. Its squares fade after playing; double-click the Comb bar to delete the last row. Comb rows never loop by themselves.

The upper corner square is the Comb's repeat switch. Tap it to loop the selected pitch blocks, or hold it to choose how many beats apart the rows fall; its small number shows the subdivision. Without a Comb row it repeats all pitches every 1/8 beat. Tap the corner again to stop just that repeating grid; your one-shot rows and other notes stay. To change a running grid's pitch blocks, switch it off and on after choosing a different Comb block. It uses one quieter audio clip, not a layer, and never turns Audio on for you.

A Workbox can be tucked without playing or deleting it; return to add notes before dropping, or choose New empty box to start fresh. The last eight sent copies (not one-pitch rail notes) remain in Saved drops inside expanded Layers during the session. Files can play independently from their channel strip or become layers within the layer limits. Automata has its own live Play/Pause; its Drop sends a finite phrase from the prepared seed. Tools and preparation edits keep existing playback running.
