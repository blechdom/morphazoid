Playing & model notes

Change ρ to explore stable equilibria and chaotic orbits. Clarity changes the sonification.

Regime changes the system; Clarity blends its raw motion with a pitched voice. Nudge changes its current state. Audio and motion have separate switches.

Up to sixteen playheads read the actual contour. Height, horizontal position, depth, center distance, angle, bend or path position can map independently to pitch, travel, loudness and pan. Growth and travel have separate clocks. Uncheck Grow shape to hold the contour while the heads keep playing.

Shape rhythms turns the heads into plucks, bells, ticks or low pulses. Tempo follows a chosen dimension around Base tempo, with optional explicit ratios. Tempo map shows each head's actual BPM and beat phase; its up/down gestures change Tempo range, or Base tempo when set to Constant. Pause freezes all clocks after release.

Wave cycles overlays the synthesized cycles. Sweep splits selects period-doubling and moves from repeating cycles into chaos. Pitch, output, Audio and Play are preserved; Pause also holds the sweep. Editing Regime or choosing a preset ends the sweep.
