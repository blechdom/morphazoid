witches brew

An interactive witch, cauldron, instrument cupboard and haunted tree. Use the picture or its keyboard shortcuts to play.
?

The night starts still and quiet. Click the picture to fade into the brew. Pull her hair back to hush the sound and hold everything still; your next click wakes it again, on the ground or in flight.

Stir the ladle to shape her singing. Spoon height chooses the voice; click her mouth for another phrase. Pull her nose for pitch and resonance, stretch her burned hat for vocal texture, or bend her spell-casting fingers. Click her forearm sleeve to cycle the hand animations. Click her eyes for an orange electrical hex.

Click an instrument on the cupboard or tree to hear its sound bite and fade into new settings as it dives into the cauldron. The giant mushrooms choose another whole preset. The twenty-sided die beside the pot randomizes all the settings. Mushrooms, moon, presets and the die refill the ingredients. Settings never launch or land the witch: only you choose when she flies.

Click the moon or leaning broom to fly; click either again to land. Pull the noodles growing from her hair for large delay bends. Touch and pull the green-and-pink fabric behind the moon to catch and filter the wind. The mushrooms and die stay within reach in flight.

Touch branches for L-system notes and a new tree shape, the spider for a groan, and its web for low plucks. Catch the cat, ceiling lizard or wall-crawling mouse to add them to the potion.

On the picture: Space wakes or hushes; D rolls the die; A cycles hand animations; H features fingers; M toggles flight; B lands; W chooses the next spell; F catches wind; V changes her phrase; X shocks her eyes; T plays and reshapes the tree; S wakes the spider; P plucks the web; C/N/L catch cat/mouse/lizard. Hold 1–5 and use arrows to bend fingers. Q/E/R add Gesticules, Rattlesnake and Throatazoid. [ and ] select an ingredient; Enter sends it to the pot. G / Shift+G change ghost tempo. Escape hushes the spell.
