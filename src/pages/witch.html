Playing notes

Enable Audio to listen. Voice and fingers keep brewing together. The eighteen real instruments on the left shelf play short sound bites and fade the scene into a new preset as they fly into the cauldron. BLECH MIDI, Syrinx and Creaturazoid sing from the tree before diving. Choose another preset to refill the shelf and tree. Click her mouth to cycle singing phrases; the spoon height also chooses her voice. Pull her nose for pitch and resonance, her burned hat in any direction for vocal texture, and her hair for a longer scratchy tail. Click her eyes for an orange electrical hex. Her right hand features the instrumental fingers. In flight, touch the green-and-pink fabric behind the moon for wind, and pull it to filter the sound. Touch branches to play the L-system synth and reshape the tree, the spider for a groan, and the web for low, unsettling plucks. Click the leaning broom or moon to fly; click either again to land. The forest fades away while her arms, hands, hair and broom travel together. Her longer route circles back through smaller loops. Pull the denser noodles growing from her hair for stronger delay bends. BLECH MIDI visitors sometimes fly past with their sound. The giant right mushrooms change the whole preset: sounds, finger animation, colors and scenery. They remain playable in flight and keep her airborne. Catch the cat, mouse or lizard to send it into the potion. The cat still meows. On the picture: H features fingers; M toggles flight; B lands; W chooses the next whole spell; F catches wind in the flying fabric; V changes her phrase; X shocks her eyes; T plays and reshapes the tree; S wakes the spider; P plucks the web; C/N/L catch cat/mouse/lizard. Hold 1–5 and use arrows to bend fingers. Q/E/R add Gesticules, Rattlesnake and Throatazoid. [ and ] select an ingredient; Enter sends it to the pot. G / Shift+G change ghost tempo. Space starts or stops; Escape lands and releases. Parameters contains keyboard-accessible alternatives.
