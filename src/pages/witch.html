Playing notes

Enable Audio to listen. Voice and fingers brew together. Presets change the whole spell and refill the shelf. Click a real Morphazoid icon on a shelf: it chooses its spell, flies into the pot and plays a featured sound. Used icons stay gone until another preset is selected. The spoon height chooses five singing characters. Pull her nose to bend pitch and resonance; tug her hat for vocal texture. Pull her hair away from her head for scratchy noise; push it back to soften it. Her hair streams and knots in flight. Touch her right palm to feature the finger instruments. Each spell pairs a Gesticules animation with its own instrumental sound. Click the broom or moon to fly: both arms and hands come along. Pull or push the snaking flight string to stretch the delay; drag the cloth in the wind to filter the entire brew. A larger black Quadruped cat crosses every thirty seconds of Play with real foot sounds and a preset-owned gait. Click the walking cat to make it meow; C does the same with the picture focused. The ghost portraits keep a quiet beat; drag them up or down to change tempo. Mushrooms change colors and bend the scene. On the picture: H features fingers; M flies; B lands; W touches mushrooms. Hold 1–5 and use arrows to bend fingers. Q/E/R add Gesticules, Rattlesnake and Throatazoid. [ and ] select a shelf icon; Enter sends it to the potion. G / Shift+G change ghost tempo; T/S/V play bats, stars and wind. Space starts or stops; Escape lands and releases. Parameters contains keyboard-accessible alternatives for the hair, cloth, flight string, cat and hands.
