About & controls

Turn Audio on to hear your MIDI through the piano SoundFont. With Loop on, tap a stamp to start it and tap again to stop just that stamp. With Loop off, stamps play once; another tap retriggers. Send copies your prepared drawing into a reusable pad below the output. Each saved pad has its own Loop switch. Later edits leave its snapshot playing. Height sets time in beats; width and horizontal offset place notes across the pitch range.

Your pads keeps the last eight sends until you leave the page, replacing the oldest stopped pad when full. Stop all and Audio off stop playback but keep those pads ready to relaunch. Up to four patterns play together; a fifth replaces the oldest playing pattern. Sync to tempo follows the tempo knob. Turning the main Loop switch off lets running loops finish their current pass. Space outside controls sends the prepared drawing or selected rule.
