About & controls

All pads and Automatapoeia share the tempo knob. Each pad has its own Loop switch: tap to start or stop a loop; one-shots retrigger on each tap. Loop drawing applies only to your next Send. Playing pads stay visible when you filter the bank.

Draw below the live output, then Send ↑ to play an independent copy and save its pad. Edit opens a copy in the preparation canvas. MIDI files and captures are rasterized into the 48 × 32 drawing grid for editing; the original pad stays intact. Width, Height and Offset affect your next drawing or stamp.

Automatapoeia evolves continuously, one row from the previous row. Its Play/Pause preserves the current generation and stops only its own sound. Restart returns to the seed. Capture saves the recent 1–16 beats as a stopped pad. Rule and rate changes affect the next generation. Pick a drawing row, then Use row as seed to restart evolution from those pitches without changing its Play/Pause state.

Up to four pads play alongside the live generator. A fifth pad replaces the oldest playing pad. The last eight saved sends remain until you leave the page; the oldest stopped saved pad is replaced when full. Stop all stops pads and pauses Automatapoeia. Audio is armed separately.
