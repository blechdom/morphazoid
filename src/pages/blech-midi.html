About & controls

Turn Audio on to hear your MIDI through the piano SoundFont. Send copies your prepared drawing into the live output; later edits leave that copy playing. Stamps and messages send immediately. Loop repeats active patterns, and Sync to tempo makes them follow the tempo knob. Stop clears the active patterns. Up to four patterns can play together; a fifth replaces the oldest.

Audio off stops active stamps. The preparation canvas stays ready. Drawings use arrow keys and Space or Enter; the live flow accepts Enter to send the selected stamp. Space elsewhere sends the prepared drawing or selected rule.
