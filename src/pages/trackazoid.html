Keys & help

A / B select a deck · Space play/pause · 1–4 hot cues · L loop · Esc pause both.

Shift-click a cue to clear it. Set Source BPM and the downbeat before using Sync or beat loops. Drag the waveform to seek; drag loop edges to trim.

For separate monitoring, choose Split cue and use a DJ splitter cable: master on left, cue on right (both mono). Audio starts off.
