Turn Audio on, then play a deck. Classic shows two records, Pro shows two waveforms, Eight decks shows every platter, and Prepare focuses on one source. Hidden decks keep playing.

The crown chooses the tempo master. Each other deck can follow it with SYNC. Choose tempo-only or beat alignment in the master options. Imported audio is analyzed; uncertain tempo falls back to an editable 120 BPM.

Drag a record or waveform forward and backward to scratch. Enable Slip to return to the uninterrupted timeline on release. M mutes and S solos without stopping playback.

Set cue stores a return point. Hold Return to cue to preview a paused deck, or jump a playing deck back without stopping it. Files play through once.

Assign each deck to A, B or Thru in its controls. The crossfader blends the two groups; Thru stays audible. Gain trims source level separately from the volume fader. Auto Gain matches sources when loaded.

Key Lock preserves pitch while changing speed. Pitch shifts the voice independently. The gear opens larger controls. Space toggles all decks; keys 1–8 toggle individual decks outside controls.
