8 DECKS

Turn Audio on, then play a deck or Play all. The eight original demo loops are ready to go.

The crown chooses the tempo master. Each other track can follow it with SYNC, or keep its own tempo. SYNC ALL toggles all followers. Set imported audio’s Source BPM and cue on a beat first. Speed changes pitch too.

Drag a record or a track’s waveform forward and backward to scratch. Release to resume its previous play state. Arrow keys scratch; Shift makes smaller movements. Play/Pause sits in the center. M mutes and S solos without stopping the record.

The fixed needle reads the loop at 3 o’clock. The entire selected loop wraps around the record. Use Track view to choose a beat loop or the whole track; all tracks repeat. The position slider seeks. The flag saves the cue and beat zero; Return to cue returns there and pauses.

Each gear opens file loading, speed, volume and beat-grid controls. Loading a file onto a playing deck asks before replacing it. Switching views keeps playback and the mix.

In the mixer, Stereo shape moves all eight tracks together: left/right is pan, up/down is volume. Arrange an octagon, then move, rotate or stretch it; drag a numbered point to edit one track. Expand it for larger controls. Undo restores the last gesture.

Space toggles all decks. Keys 1–8 toggle individual decks outside controls.
