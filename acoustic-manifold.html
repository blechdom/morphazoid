What “multiscale” and “multifractal” mean

Multiscale analysis asks how a measurement changes when it is viewed through several time windows—for example, fine pulses, mid-length motifs, and whole bouts. This prototype combines frame, event, and sequence scales and summarizes envelope variation at several window sizes.

Multifractal analysis is a narrower statistical claim: different parts of a signal exhibit different scaling exponents across a sufficiently broad range of scales. Establishing that normally needs a method such as multifractal detrended fluctuation analysis, enough suitable data, an explicit scale range, uncertainty estimates, and comparison with shuffled or phase-randomized surrogate signals.

The present map does not estimate a multifractal spectrum. Multiple envelope windows, PCA, or a single fractal dimension are not by themselves evidence of multifractality.

See Roeske et al. on multifractal rhythm in thrush nightingale song for an analysis that includes multiscale statistics and surrogate tests.

Method, vocabulary, and references

Standardized cepstral and spectral frames form a deterministic projection trajectory; each node sits at the centroid of one detected occurrence. Dotted nearest-neighbor edges summarize acoustic resemblance. Solid arrows preserve recording order.

Strophe is used only by the songbird profile for a continuous vocal bout bounded by a conspicuous pause. Other profiles call the same graph unit an event, call, phrase, or chirp. An occurrence is not automatically a learned repertoire type.

Profiles tune operational boundaries and playback mappings; they are not classifiers. This is a graph of occurrences in the analyzed window, not proof of species identity, grammar, a complete repertoire, or a small-world network.

Source-derived audio plays mono event slices with short edge fades and contains samples from the recording. PCM WAV is parsed directly at its source sample rate; other formats use browser decoding and may be resampled. Model sketch sends extracted trajectories through a reduced synthesis model and contains no source samples. Its gesture clock, pitch, resonant-body scale, and texture are independently transformable; a separate control can exaggerate node differences by mapping PC1 to pitch, PC2 to texture, and PC3 to speed. Those PCA mappings are explicitly artistic because the axes are dataset-dependent. Infrasonic and ultrasonic profiles first use a disclosed frequency-scaled mapping for audibility. This is parameter sonification and extrapolation, not recovery of a unique biological mechanism or proof of an animal's physiological limit.
