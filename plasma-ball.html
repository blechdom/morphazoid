No scale or tuning grid is imposed. Each discharge flickers, drops out, and restrikes instead of sustaining a constant drone.

The central electrode sends intermittent synth voices along branching static discharges throughout a three-dimensional glass globe. Pointer or touch contact attracts only some nearby endpoints; the remaining arcs continue searching other near and far surfaces. The Speed and intensity slider scales a simultaneous mix of slow and fast filament rates together with discharge force. Arc angle maps continuously to free pitch, each short static impulse maps to amplitude and brightness, and depth changes distance, tone, and stereo position. Use Space to play or pause, Left and Right Arrow to move a keyboard attractor, Enter to surge, and Escape to switch audio off.
