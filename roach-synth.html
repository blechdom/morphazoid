Specimen & sound sources

Real cockroach scan by Kathrin&Christian, CC BY 4.0. Morphazoid adds an approximate rigid rig with 31 runtime joints. These are stylized gestures from a dried specimen, not a validated walking model.

Two textured covers and two authored hindwings move independently. The long antennae have joints; small mouth feelers remain part of the head. Hiss, scraping and vibration inspire the sound layers. The talking robo-bug is fictional.
