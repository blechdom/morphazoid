How does it say a word?
  1. The local dictionary turns spelling into speech phones.
  2. Each phone selects tongue, constriction, lip, velum, voicing, and noise targets.
  3. Consonants keep discrete closures and releases; continuous lanes coarticulate around them.
  4. The Throatazoid worklet propagates a glottal pulse through the resulting 44-section tract.
