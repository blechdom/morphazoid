Play the fractal's outlines

Enable Audio, then press Play. In Contour Explorer, Trace follows three detail layers together. In the XY oscillator and other demos, the left speaker reads X and the right reads Y; the moving dot demonstrates that scan 256 times slower.

Julia Vectors: counter-rotating complex vectors form a Julia equipotential. Try the circle, ellipse, basilica and rabbit. A circle and ellipse both contain only the fundamental with angle traversal: a different-looking shape need not have more harmonics.

Mandelbrot Contours: an inverse exterior map outlines the whole Mandelbrot set, not just its cardioid. Decrease clearance to expose its bulbs. Finite detail approximates a smooth surrounding contour, not the exact fractal boundary.

Bulb Voyager: the small diagram shows a parameter travelling inside the main cardioid or period-two bulb. Its changing Julia contour makes a continuously changing waveform. A precomputed bank interpolates between 48 contours.

Contour Explorer: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, or choose a region such as Seahorse Valley. New vectors are computed as you move, with a quick preview followed by finer detail. Arrow keys pan, +/− zoom, and Home restores the whole view. Edge detail moves the contour closer to the set; Fine increases the final refinement.

Trace: Broad, Curl and Fine show three sampled contour levels, each with its own colored head and voice. They share one cycle length; different path lengths mean the heads need not reach matching curls together. Height controls continuous pitch, left/right position pans, and bending changes brightness. Choose Sine, Reed or Glass for each level; uncheck a layer to hear the others alone. Broad plays a lower register and Fine a higher one.

Cycle rate controls travel independently of Pitch. Feature size chooses how much of the path is used to measure a bend: small values respond to tiny curls, larger values to broader turns. Click a fine strand or use Next strand to follow its neighborhood across the levels. A level outside the visible region is silent. These are Mandelbrot detail levels, not recursive Julia pullbacks.

Closed paths loop; cropped open paths scan smoothly there and back. The heads follow the audio clock and keep their cycle through voice and detail edits. Existing sound continues during refinement. No contours in the completed region means silence. Choose XY oscillator to use the selected outline as one audio waveform instead. Save SVG includes all three completed levels in Trace.

These are sampled exterior contours. Extremely thin connections may be missed, so small apparent islands are not a certified topology. Zoom is bounded by floating-point precision. Save SVG exports the current completed vectors.

Traversal changes how fast different parts of the same path are read. Pitch also reduces the available detail to stay below Nyquist. The colored path shows the filtered XY waveform; the faint source retains visual detail. Both axes share one gain, and geometric translation is removed to avoid DC. In mono, the colored XY path collapses to X = Y.

Turning off Vectors or Source contour affects only the display. Presets and Reset never arm Audio or change master level. These are playable mathematical approximations, not sampled instruments.
