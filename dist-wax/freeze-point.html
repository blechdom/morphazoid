How it works

Each cell is a resonator. Coupling only moves energy between cells that are close in frequency, and Disorder detunes them apart — so past a threshold the transfer collapses and energy stops travelling. Strike a cell below the threshold and the whole grid lights up; strike it above and the light stays where you put it.

The readout measures the participation ratio — roughly how many cells are sharing the energy — and locates the threshold for the current coupling. It is computed from the model, not asserted.

Record feeds your own take into the input cell on a loop, so the phenomenon acts on your sound rather than on a test tone. Shift-click to move where the microphone enters.

Honesty: a two-dimensional coupled-resonator model showing localization behaviour. Not a claim of Anderson-localization fidelity, and no critical exponents are asserted.
