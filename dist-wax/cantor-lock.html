Method / what this means

Finite analogy

A fractal uncertainty principle gives a quantitative obstruction to simultaneous concentration on porous sets in a variable and its Fourier-dual variable. Cantor Lock makes one finite matrix analogy audible: it searches for the vector that retains the most energy after both masks, then sonifies the retained glass lattice and its amber leakage.

The computed singular value belongs only to this finite sampled operator. It is not the theorem’s exponent, not a proof, and not evidence from quantum hardware.
