Playing notes

Enable Audio to listen. Voice and fingers brew together. Drag the spoon upward or downward through five singing characters; its automatic motion chooses them too. Pull her nose or hat to change her singing. Lift the glowing ember to brighten her finger spells and night accents. Her right hand stays enlarged; touch its palm to bring the finger sound forward. Drop the hovering ingredients and miniature Morphazoids into the pot for short solos. Glass adds ringing resonance; hair adds unstable harmonics. Open Parameters for the complete ingredient list and keyboard controls. The moon launches her looping broom and sends the whole brew through a wild delay. The mushrooms change colors and bend the lines. On the picture: H brings the fingers forward in the mix; M launches or rings the moon; B lands; W touches mushrooms. Hold 1–5 and use arrows to bend fingers. Q/E/R drop the hand, marimba and singing mouth. Use [ and ] to select any ingredient, then Enter to drop it. Use - and + to change brightness. T/G/S/V play night spirits. Space starts or stops; Escape lands and releases.
