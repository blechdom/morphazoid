Family

FM / PM · linear / warped

Recursive FM

Linear signal → frequency. Each sine drives the next oscillator’s rate.

Recursive PM

Linear signal → phase. Each sine displaces the next oscillator’s phase.

Chaotic FM

Tanh signal → signed frequency. The nonlinear signal sets the next rate.

Chaotic PM

Tanh-shaped signal → phase. Nonlinear feedback displaces the next oscillator without breaking phase periodicity.

A live non-scrolling log-frequency spectrum and foreground oscilloscope sit above the Chaotic PM turn. In Smooth mode, the previous sine is continuously tanh-shaped, mixed by the chaos amount, applied as phase displacement in radians, and sent through the next sine. Legacy Raw mode preserves the original signed-remainder experiment. The final turn is audible.
