The act

01 / MOTION

16 beats: verse → fill → break → refrain. Rest and hold beats leave musical gaps.

Wildness adds off-target throws you can rescue. Set it to zero for precise juggling. The crowd lobs replacements after misses.

The sound

03 / SONIFICATION

Each catch punches its drum through the riffs.
Each flight plays its object's riff.
Height bends pitch; motion changes rhythm and grit. Drops bring the boos.

Physics & view

Mass changes drag, spin, and impact. Tempo and loft rescale the show's gravity to keep fast throws high. Drop a prop and the audience lobs another onto the stage. Throw one back with G: the crowd catches it and returns a different object. Ride under the returning arc.

Backstage / references

An unofficial procedural likeness of Puggler from Xavier: Renegade Angel. Roxy and Moss are original punk / rave partners.

Kick Man (1981) contributes the unicycle, balloon stack, and rescue kick.

Phrases use compatible siteswap chunks. The evolving set composes new verse, fill, break, and refrain sections. Our one-to-ten-object ensemble adapts passing ideas from the Passing Patterns Compendium.

Acoustic drums and audience voices are credited in sample credits. Guitar, bass, and “oi” are original synthesized riffs. No music or dialogue from the show is used.
