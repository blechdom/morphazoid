    Syntax

    examples

    y = x^2, sin(2x) or f(x) = e^-x cos(x) draw functions of x; x = y^2 draws a function of y.

    x^2 + y^2 = 4 is an implicit curve. y < x^2 shades a region.

    r = cos(3θ) is polar (type theta for θ). (cos t, sin t) is parametric. (a, b) is a point you can drag when a and b are sliders.

    a = 2 makes a slider. Using an undefined letter offers to add one. 2 + 2 just shows its value.

    Functions: sin cos tan asin acos atan sinh cosh tanh ln log exp sqrt cbrt abs floor ceil round sign min max mod, and !. Constants: π (pi), τ (tau), e.

    Notes, limits and the sonification plan

    Drag to pan. Wheel, pinch, plus or minus zoom around the pointer. Home resets the view. Arrow keys pan. Space plays or pauses the reader. Drag a highlighted point to change the sliders that define it.
