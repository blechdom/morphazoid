Isohedral plane divisions use Craig S. Kaplan’s TactileJS library (project page, BSD-3-Clause). Hyperbolic presets are generated locally from Poincaré geodesic reflections; no Escher artwork is embedded.

Tap a shape to select its actual outline. Shape follows that contour, Neighbors continues across shared borders, and Pattern follows matching contours in the tessellation. Drag with one pointer to pan and pinch with two pointers to zoom. View buttons also zoom or reset the view. Press Enter or Space to play or pause the selected outline, use the Arrow keys to move the selection to an actual neighboring shape, press Plus or Minus to zoom, Home to reset the view, G to show or hide the geometry scaffold, and Escape to switch audio off.
