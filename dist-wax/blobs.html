Drawing & keyboard help

Pencil closes on release. Pen: click for a corner, or drag to pull out curve handles. Lines: click each corner. After three points, the dashed loop previews with playheads and sound when Audio and Play are on. Return to the first point or use Close loop to keep it; Cancel discards the preview.

Edit: drag inside a blob to move the whole shape. Drag outside the blobs to rotate the entire drawing around the canvas center. Drag a point or its curve handles to reshape the contour. Double-click a line to add a point without changing the pen curve; double-click a point to delete it. Alt-drag a handle to bend one side independently. Make corner removes handles; Smooth point adds them. Roundness reflows between points when you add or remove one.

Focus the canvas. Arrow keys move the cursor; Shift moves faster. Enter adds points; C closes a loop; Escape cancels. In Edit, Tab cycles points, arrows move the selected point, Enter adds a point after it, and Delete/Backspace removes it. Alt+arrow keys move the whole selected blob; [ and ] rotate all blobs (Shift turns farther). A loop keeps at least three points. Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes; Shift Ctrl/⌘ Z redoes. Space plays or pauses. With MIDI enabled, computer piano keys take priority.
