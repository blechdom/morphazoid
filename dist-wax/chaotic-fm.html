CHAOTIC SYNTHS · 03

Chaotic FM

Each oscillator’s waveform becomes the next oscillator’s signed frequency, shaped through amount, tanh saturation, and rate stages. Unlike regular FM’s predictable sidebands, tiny phase and timing changes reshape every downstream frequency trajectory. Saturation keeps this recursive cascade bounded while producing highly sensitive, unstable, and surprising spectral motion.
