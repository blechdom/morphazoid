Method, provenance, and references

Standardized MFCC and spectral frames form a deterministic PCA trajectory; each strophe node sits at its trajectory centroid. Dotted nearest-neighbor edges use a separate standardized descriptor spanning duration, rhythm, spectral summaries, trajectory spread, and three envelope scales. Solid arrows preserve the observed recording order.

The display preserves silence-gated active runs inside each strophe as tone candidates. They are not verified fundamentals, notes, syllables, or species labels; bead color uses each short frame’s dominant spectral-bin peak.

The browser recipe uses 12 MFCCs plus seven frame descriptors. It is a compact, Arese-inspired approximation, not a reproduction of Seeing Birdsong’s 40-MFCC feature pipeline.

Fine, mid, and broad envelope variation is measured at roughly 11, 43, and 171 ms at the target analysis rate. This is a compact multiscale summary, not a claim of multifractal estimation.

This is a graph of occurrences in one short recording, not a typed repertoire or a small-world claim. Cluster colors are exploratory and do not assign established song types.

Recording plays slices of the source and therefore contains samples. Physical sends extracted trajectories through a resynthesis model and contains no source samples. Neither reconstructs a unique biological mechanism.
