Reference signal Pink noise · −20 dBFS rms

Turn down first. These are digital reference levels: pink noise is −20 dBFS RMS; tone and chirp are −18 dBFS peak. Your OS volume, interface, amplifier, and room determine the resulting SPL. Confirm every numbered output by ear. LFE tests are band-limited (the tone is 80 Hz) with no digital +10 dB boost.

Patch + record

input · stems

ready · 15 s maximum

Loaded audio is downmixed once to mono, enters at unity, and follows the coral source through the spatial patch. Recording exports synchronized mono WAV stems in one ZIP, tapped before the operating system, output device, and DAC. To capture the analog speaker feeds, use hardware or system loopback.
