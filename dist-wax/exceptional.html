How it works

Two modes at the same centre frequency, coupled by kappa, with balanced gain and loss. Their separation is the square root of kappa² − gamma²: as the balance rises the two partials converge, and at gamma = kappa they do not merely meet — they coalesce. That is the exceptional point.

The identity is the square root. Away from the point, Nudge moves the sound in proportion. Near it, the same nudge moves it enormously, because the response goes as √ε rather than ε. The readout shows that number changing as you approach.

Past the point the split becomes imaginary: one frequency, two different decay rates, one of them growing. Gain is clamped below self-oscillation and the output is limited.
