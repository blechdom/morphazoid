How it works

Each edge is a synapse with two states: facilitation, which rises with every pulse and decays between them, and available resources, which each pulse depletes and which recover slowly. Effective strength is their product, which makes every edge band-pass in pulse rate — weak when you play too slowly, weak when you play too fast, strong in between.

The seven ring steps facilitate slowly and prefer unhurried playing. The seven shortcuts facilitate and deplete quickly and prefer fast bursts. So tempo is the routing control: sweep Pulse rate and the signal physically moves between the two subnetworks. Mutual inhibition decides whether a branch point passes everything or only its strongest edge.

This is why it exists: in earlier prototypes the branch decision came from a rotor, whose period turned out to be fixed by topology and unsteerable. Here the decision comes from how you play, so it is under your hands.

Honesty: this is a musical model using the standard facilitation/depression form. It is not a claim of biological accuracy, not a neuron simulation, and the "lattice" of the referenced audio plugin is not reproduced here.
