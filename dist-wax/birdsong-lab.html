What this POC is actually doing

A YIN-style periodicity analysis estimates pitch and voicing. Frame RMS becomes an airflow-pressure proxy; log pitch becomes a tension proxy. Those curves drive the same nonlinear bilateral labial oscillator used by Morphazoid’s Syrinx instrument, followed by two damped vocal-tract resonances. Everything runs in this browser tab.

This demonstrates a manipulable sound-producing equivalent. It does not recover unique anatomy, muscle activity, or true air-sac pressure from sound alone.
