CC routes & key map

Choose what each controller knob moves. The Generic profile uses CC14–21; the MIDI Map in Settings adapts other controllers.

Combine keys into poses. Octaves vary the pose and pitch; velocity changes strength, pitch bend moves ±2 semitones, and sustain holds notes.

Every key plays its body part’s assigned sound. Program Change selects a sound preset. Notes never start or stop either player.

Poses + travel maps the twelve pitch classes around the web: C moves forward, D♯ right, F♯ back and A left. Octaves and velocity change speed; release keys to stop steering. Sustain holds the pose, not travel.

CC1 movement · CC7 level · CC10 pan · CC11 expression · CC64 sustain · CC71 body · CC74 brightness. Assigned controller macros take precedence. MIDI Start/Stop controls animation; incoming clock sets tempo. C4 = note 60.

Computer keys: Z S X D C V G B H N J M = C3–B3; Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U = C4–B4. [ / ] shift octaves.

Specimen & sound sources

A real Argiope bruennichi scan by Yuichi Kano / ffish.asia, CC0. Morphazoid adds a flexible rig with eight articulated legs and authored face pivots. The head and thorax are fused; tiny mouthpart boundaries are approximate.

The web is a musical string model: shorter silk subdivisions ring higher; tension raises pitch, and leg speed and angle shape each pluck. These are amplified, stylized string sounds, not recordings of a spider’s voice.
