Model & sources

research notes

A model of acoustics, not a model of a culture. Khöömei is living Tuvan and Mongolian heritage with distinct lineages, aesthetics, and teaching practices. The named buttons are audible starting points, not claims of authentic performance.

foldsLF-like pulse + period division → airway44 variable-area tube sections → focusconverging F2 / F3 resonances
  • Sygyt: dynamic MRI and transmission-line modeling support two tract constrictions and a narrow merged-formant peak that selects one source harmonic.
  • Kargyraa: high-speed imaging supports ventricular folds closing once for every two true-fold cycles, producing period doubling rather than a separate bass oscillator.
  • False folds vary: a 2025 endoscopic study across 18 Inner Mongolian Hoomei productions found contacting false folds important in some productions and confirmed posterior-tract narrowing in an examined production.
  • Breathing: aspiration is exhaled noise during voice; the release sound here is an open-glottis, voiceless intake. Inspiratory phonation is a separate technique with reversed airflow and different fold motion.
  • Western overtone singing: related F2/F3 clustering can arise from different articulatory strategies, so it remains a comparison rather than a Tuvan preset.
  • Mongolian Khöömei: a related but distinct living tradition includes deep and whistled families; this page does not relabel its Tuvan Xöömei starting point as Mongolian.
  • Inuit katajjaq: a paired, alternating throat-song game is structurally different from this solo sustained-drone model, so it is intentionally not reduced to a preset here.
  • This browser model: a Pink-Trombone-lineage waveguide is paired with explicit F2/F3 resonators and an optional experimental sublingual branch. It is intentionally interpretable, not a biometric copy.

This synthesizer does not teach vocal technique. Real pressed and ventricular-fold phonation can be fatiguing; learn with a qualified tradition-bearer or voice professional and stop if phonation hurts.

Use the harmonic buttons or number keys 4 through 9 to choose an overtone. On the canvas, arrow keys move the overtone focus; hold Shift for larger steps. Space begins or releases the drone. Release plays a voiceless breath intake when Audible inhale is above zero; press I to audition it. This is not inspiratory phonation. Keys A, S, D, F, G, H, J, and K select drone notes.
