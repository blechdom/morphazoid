Start with Audio on and forward Play: REC prints the loaded track into one shared loop, and blue PLAY heads read it after the measured travel time. Drag PLAY 01 to hear its delay change. In MOVE, drag an existing post to move just that post, or pull bare tape for one new bend. Choose DRAW to trace multiple bends from bare tape in one stroke. TIME toggles the display only; New Pool adds stored seconds. Razor adds two differently timed virtual routes and a movable blue join. Drag that join onto a later intact stretch before a playhead to Scotch it there as a mixer, or choose Scotch and touch that stretch. On the focused desk, R selects Razor, T Scotch, G Move, D Draw, E Eraser, brackets select a part or either branch, arrows move it, Delete erases the selection and Enter applies the tool. Space plays or pauses.

What changes the sound?
  • Audio / ▶ deck Audio enables sound; ▶ moves the tape. STOP silences it.
  • REC / PLAY heads In forward Play, red REC writes the loaded track plus any selected PLAY-head return into one shared looping tape memory. More RECs overdub that same loop at other positions: they do not create tracks, capture your mic or send to a separate output. Blue PLAY heads read the loop into the stereo monitor; set Monitor to 100% tape to hear only them. The masthead Record captures the stereo output separately.
  • MOVE / DRAW / TIME MOVE drags one post or adds one bend from bare tape. DRAW traces multiple posts from bare tape. TIME only displays seconds; moving a post or adding a POOL changes travel time and sound.
  • RAZOR / TAPE Touch tape after REC and before PLAY with Razor to make two differently timed reads. Scotch rejoins them later as an A/B mixer, or restores one path.

This desk allows 32 added posts, 6 pools, 4 PLAY and 3 REC heads. Pools share a 12-second loop; heads use bounded real-time audio contacts.
