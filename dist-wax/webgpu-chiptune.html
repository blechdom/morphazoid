The active Shadertoy sound path is translated from GLSL to WGSL and rendered in bounded stereo chunks. Musically meaningful active-path values are editable; numerical stability constants, 12-TET math, BLEP calibration, sample rate, and the safety ceiling remain fixed. Dormant source experiments are intentionally omitted.

A WebGPU compute shader renders a procedural tracker composition with two upper pulse voices, bass, lead, arpeggio, noise percussion, drums, and ping-pong echo into Web Audio.

Five original code-drawn, low-resolution game characters share the shader's master transport. Their feet and poses follow each voice's sequence clock and current step. Level changes size and brightness; register and notes change height and color; waveform, pulse shape, articulation, and voice-specific timbre change each silhouette and movement. Rests keep the character visible in an idle pose. Echo makes afterimages, kick compresses the floor, snare throws blocks, and hats and shaker make edge sparks.

Upper A is a cyan scout. Upper B is a lime mechanical runner. Bass is a purple stomping monster. Lead is a pink caped hero. Arp is an amber winged sprite.

Focus the tracker and use left or right arrow keys to change its deterministic pattern seed. Use up or down arrow keys to change pitch range. Hold Shift for larger changes.

In Sequence view, choose a voice. Left and right move between steps. Enter or Space cycles Auto, Note, and Rest. A, N, and R select a state. Up and down change a manual value; Shift changes by 12 semitones for pitched voices or 0.1 for the Arp contour. Delete restores Auto.
