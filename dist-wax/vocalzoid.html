06How the old machines sang

Classic Vocaloid libraries were mostly diphones plus sustained vowels. Yamaha documented about 2,000 samples per recorded pitch; one published example recorded three pitch ranges. Its renderer corrected phase and spectral shape across joins — more than an ordinary audio fade.

UTAU separates pitch/time resampling from WAV concatenation. Its oto.ini stores the sample offset, fixed consonant region, cutoff, preutterance, and overlap. Vocalzoid reads those six timings but uses a deliberately lightweight Web Audio renderer, so it is neither Vocaloid-compatible nor a replacement for OpenUtau.
