Playing the loom

Each cord remembers a recording. Its two knots enclose a repeating passage. Slide a knot to change the passage; pull the tassel down to lengthen its time. Stretch keeps approximately the same pitch, with a grainy texture at extremes.

Click a cord to move the playback bead. Choose Tie to another cord, then click a destination cord: the bead leaves at the last knot and enters the next passage. Untie restores the cord’s own loop. Reverse reads upward.

Enable the microphone, then Record phrase. Press again to finish; recording stops at 30 seconds. Load a file under 3 minutes and 40 MB to use its first 30 seconds. Recordings stay in this page only. Undo restores the last eight edits or takes.

On the loom: 1–3 select cords; ↑/↓ move the upper knot; Shift + ↑/↓ move the lower knot; ←/→ stretch; R reverses; T begins a tie; Escape cancels; Enter moves the bead. Space plays or pauses. The labelled controls provide the same actions.

Inspired by the fictional knotted time-navigation device described in The Rise and Fall of D.O.D.O. The musical mappings are invented for this instrument.
