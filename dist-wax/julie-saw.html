Acoustics, techniques + model limits ↗

Drag the pink flex hand horizontally to change the arch and pitch and vertically to change the tip curl. Drag the amber bow left and right across the smooth edge to bow; moving vertically changes contact position and pointer pressure changes force. Two simultaneous pointers can operate both arms. Use A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P and semicolon as chromatic notes through the shared computer-key player. Space toggles the automatic phrase, Escape silences the instrument, and arrow keys flex the blade while the canvas is focused. The Bow and exciter buttons and sliders provide keyboard-accessible equivalents for every continuous gesture.
