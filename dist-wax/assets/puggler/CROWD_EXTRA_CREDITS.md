# Additional rear-view audience

`crowd-extra-collage.webp` was generated on 2026-09-10 with OpenAI's built-in
image generation tool. These are fictional photographic-style cutouts, not
photographs of identifiable real people. The original PNG was encoded as WebP
at unchanged dimensions; its generated alpha channel is preserved byte for byte.
No source photograph or third-party image was imported. Crops live in
`src/puggler-crowd-extra.js`.

Real photographic hair strands, fabric grain, soft warm stage bounce light, slightly grubby handmade collage aesthetic, not illustration or cartoon. These are generic fictional audience members.
Each cutout completely contained in its own equal third of the image, generous completely empty separation, full head and shoulders intact, no cropped hair, no overlaps. Center each subject in its cell; baby cutout visibly smaller but crisp.
Genuinely transparent background with alpha throughout all empty space. NO white background, NO checkerboard painted into the image, NO white borders, outlines, halos, sticker outlines, frames, text, labels, drop shadows, extra objects, or extra people. Only the three cutouts.
