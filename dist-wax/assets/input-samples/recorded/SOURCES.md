# Recorded input sample sources

These eight compact demos use actual freely redistributable recordings. Each primary Freesound sound page explicitly releases its recording under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credits remain here even though CC0 does not require attribution. No TV, film, commercial music, or meme-video audio was extracted.

The downloaded files were Freesound’s published high-quality MP3 previews, not the lossless originals. Complete standalone performances retain original pitch and timing. All final files are mono 44.1 kHz 128 kbps MP3 with short boundary fades and linear peak normalization to−4dBFS before encoding. The toy gamelan demo combines artist-released home-recorded notes into an original short phrase; it does not represent a traditional ensemble, composition, or ritual performance. Exact transformations, source URLs, download hashes, final hashes and decoded signal measurements appear in `provenance.json`.
