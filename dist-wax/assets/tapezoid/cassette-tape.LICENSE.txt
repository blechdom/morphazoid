The bundled cassette-tape.glb is the user's 1024-pixel-texture download,
included byte-for-byte without changing the mesh or textures. Its SHA-256 is
19f27f4eb2b0c5463623dd1479fee7e6874c3b8f2df88bd022f28ef325009668.
It has one textured mesh (3,837 vertices), three embedded images, and no
separately authored reel nodes or animation. The browser partitions six
disconnected mesh components into two rotating reels *in memory*, and renders
the plastic as an opaque depth layer with translucent windows. The distributed
GLB itself remains unchanged. The tape path, transport, ports, heads and tools
are separate Morphazoid geometry/code. An artist-model failure falls back to
the generated shell. There is no claim of endorsement by the model's creator.
