How it works

Sound sent through a scattering medium arrives smeared. Replay that smear time-reversed through the same medium and it reconverges on where it started — a real technique used in medical ultrasound and non-destructive testing.

Record a phrase and press Play scattered: ruin. Press Reverse and it walks back out and refocuses. Then drag a scatterer and reverse again — the refocus degrades, which proves the smear was information rather than noise. The quality number is measured by comparing the path set at recording time with the current one.

Honesty: a sparse-tap path model, not a multiple-scattering solver and not a measured room response. Refocusing sharpness here is illustrative.
