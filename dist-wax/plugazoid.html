Quick start

Instructions

  1. Turn on Audio with the speaker button in the top bar.
  2. Choose and load a WAM2 effect. Plugazoid downloads it directly from the official community catalogue.
  3. Choose an input: Test signal needs no permission; Connect mic needs headphones and browser approval. Use the plug-in's own controls, then Bypass to compare dry audio.

What this provesA real third-party WAM2 module, its GUI, and its DSP can be fetched at runtime and inserted into mic → plug-in → output.

What it does notThis does not load installed VST3, CLAP, or Audio Unit binaries. Native formats still need a source port or a local desktop bridge.

Where it livesThe MVP streams catalogue modules from webaudiomodules.com. A production rack should pin versions and self-host an approved subset so updates, CORS, or downtime cannot break a session.
