What is 4D here?

geometry note

A tesseract has eight cubic boundary cells. Each one carries a 3 × 3 × 3 field of color, so this puzzle has 216 stickers rather than 54.

A turn rotates one cubic cell through one of its three internal coordinate planes. The screen shows a perspective projection from 4D to 3D, then from 3D to your display.

Drag to orbit the three-dimensional shadow. Choose Fold W or hold Shift while dragging to rotate the puzzle through the fourth dimension. Use the cell and plane buttons to select a cubic boundary cell and one of its three legal turn planes, then use the minus or plus ninety-degree buttons. Press Enter on the canvas for a clockwise turn.
