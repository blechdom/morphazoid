What is 4D here?

geometry note

A tesseract has eight cubic boundary cells. Each one carries a 3 × 3 × 3 field of color, so this order-3 puzzle has 216 stickers.

The Sticker hyperbar reads those records as eight instrument rows by 27 pulses. Its sparse gates can leave a pulse empty after turns move colors through time. The continuous rattlesnake is the shared 217th voice.

Sticker stream visits all 216 stickers in a stable serial order; Corner stream follows the 64 stickers born at home-corner addresses. Each pulse sounds and lights that exact sticker. These serial modes begin without automatic twists, while the original sixteen-step Twist tape—with intentional authored rests—remains the default.

A turn rotates one cubic cell through one of its three internal coordinate planes. The screen shows a perspective projection from 4D to 3D, then from 3D to your display.

Drag to orbit the three-dimensional shadow. Choose Fold W or hold Shift while dragging to rotate the puzzle through the fourth dimension.
