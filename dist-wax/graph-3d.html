3D Graph · how to play

Start a traversal

Enable Audio, then Play for repeating injections, or Send note for one traversal from the selected node. Tempo sets injection spacing; the graph sets travel time between notes. The seed defaults to MIDI pitch 68; Continuous tuning leaves geometry-derived pitches unsnapped. MIDI supplies a reference frequency, not a scale.

Shape it, then leave it there

Drag empty space to orbit. Drag a node to move it in the screen plane; Shift-drag or Drag into depth moves along camera depth. Editing a node automatically pins it: it stays where you leave it, even with forces enabled. Use Unpin node or Unpin all to let forces move it again. X/Y/Z sliders provide precise world-axis edits.

Forces are off initially. Repulsion separates nodes; springs favor their rest length; Center attraction draws inward; Shell attraction pulls toward a sphere. Pins hold local coordinates; deliberately changing Depth spread or Twist still transforms the whole shape. These are bounded artistic layout forces, not a validated acoustic simulation.

Assign space to sound

Each Source → sound row has its own source menu and amount. You can use one axis for several destinations, or turn an assignment off. Camera rotation never retunes or repans the graph.

  • Travel time: XYZ route length, distance along X/Y/Z, arrival radius, or route stretch. Bigger values make longer waits. Minimum travel time × the mapped multiplier sets each delay, capped at 2 seconds. Off or multiplier 1 gives equal travel times.
  • Pitch: X/Y/Z position, radius, incoming route length/stretch, or accumulated signed 3D bends. Pitch span is a continuous octave range around the seed; Off holds the seed. Bends accumulate along a traversal; the bend normal’s Y component chooses the sign.
  • FM / PM color: the source adds modulation depth above the base FM/PM index. Inactive for Sine, Triangle and Shepard voices.
  • Brightness / level: lower source values become darker and slightly quieter as Shade amount increases. Off removes this shading.
  • Stereo pan: lower source values go left, higher go right. Stereo width sets the amount; Off centers the voice.

Route length means true XYZ distance, not screen length. Radius is distance from the center. Stretch compares world-space route length with the spring-rest reference. Incoming route values are sampled at departure; the initial injected note has zero incoming length/stretch. Axis positions are bipolar; distances/stretch are normalized across their range.

Routes, presets and recovery

Click an edge midpoint to switch its route, or use Selected route. Return controls affect cycles only. In-flight deadlines stay fixed; each next hop uses the current graph. Up to 120 ms of already scheduled sound may remain after a route edit.

Thirty presets explore different shapes, rhythms, voices, forces and mappings. A preset replaces graph edits, pins and route switches but keeps Audio, Play and camera state. All presets use Continuous tuning; their controls remain freely editable. Arrange resets only unpinned nodes. Reset restores the default graph and camera without disarming Audio or changing Play.

Keyboard and saving

With the stage focused: arrows orbit; [ / ] select a node; Shift+arrows move and pin it; P toggles its pin; Enter sends a note; Space toggles Play. Save/Load stores a patch in this browser, not transport or camera state. No microphone is used. Hidden pages stop and release audio.
