Every typed letter creates one formant-synth mouth with a tongue and glottis. Drag a body beyond the ring to grow and stretch it while raising pitch, lowering its formants, and increasing glottal screech. Push toward the center to crush and pinch the tract into deeper, wetter nasal noise; sweep sideways to curl the tongue and skew the cavity. Grab inside the opening and pull outward to extract its tongue. Shift-drag crushes its lips, two fingers resize it, and Alt-drag warps its complete body. Space starts or stops the looping word.
