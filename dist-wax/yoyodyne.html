This MVP edits a generated polyphonic Web Audio relay choir. Its throat, mouth, and halo colors are oscillator-and-filter approximations—not speech, vocal reconstruction, or recorded voices—and use no speech samples. It does not analyze, separate, retune, or time-stretch recorded audio.

Select a signal cell with a pointer. Keys 1 through 9 select cells 1 through 9; Page Up and Page Down move through every cell. Drag a cell body vertically to change pitch or horizontally to change start time. Drag either bright rim to change duration. On the keyboard, use Up and Down for pitch, Left and Right for time, and left or right bracket for duration. Shift makes a larger change. Enter auditions the selected cell. R recalls it. Space operates the visible transport.
