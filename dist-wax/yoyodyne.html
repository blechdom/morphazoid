Playing & model notes

Turn Audio on, then Perform for a repeating trick or Throw for one gesture. Drag the stage to move the hand. Arrow keys move the hand; Enter throws, T tugs, C catches. Space toggles Perform. A catch can stall if the yo-yo runs out of spin.

A two-dimensional, compliant-string model with coupled spin and payout. Cradle uses prescribed finger pinches. The sound is a musical string sonification, not a recording or a full simulation of yo-yo acoustics, knots or 3D gyroscopic motion.
