What this means

holes · lines · Fourier leakage

In Cohen’s higher-dimensional theorem, one concentration set is porous in balls and the other, on the Fourier side, is porous along every line; the roles may be swapped. Ordinary porosity alone is insufficient in higher dimensions. This finite probe only illustrates why a complete line can evade an ordinary-hole test—it does not verify the hypotheses or prove an uncertainty bound.
