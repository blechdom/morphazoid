Visible score

upper acid · sides drums

The upper visible face is one acid lane. The left and right visible faces are two percussion lanes. By default all three read left-to-right, top-to-bottom together; hidden stickers are silent.

Drag a visible sticker left, right, up, or down to turn its row or column. Drag empty stage space to orbit the whole form. Use arrow keys to turn the selected sticker's row or column and Space to play or pause. Random Twists uses its own movement tempo, independent of sequencer BPM. Choose Rows together, Snake together, or Alternate faces in the Clock section to change how the visible stickers are read. Alternate faces divides each beat into acid, Drum A, and Drum B subdivisions. The Geometry menu loads 2 by 2 through 6 by 6 cubes, Pyramid, or Sphere forms. The upper visible face makes acid notes while the two side faces trigger drums. Larger projected stickers sound louder according to Visibility dynamics, and any sticker that cannot be seen is silent.
