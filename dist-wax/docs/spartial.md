# SPARTIAL

**SPARTIAL** is a surround additive synthesizer built on Surround for Safety's output layouts. It starts with sixteen stationary partials and supports up to thirty-two per note. Play sustains the selected single note or chord; a chromatic keyboard and MIDI supply independent note gates. Twelve instrument presets and eight harmonic fingerprints have Next and parameter dice controls. Frequency spacing compresses or expands the harmonic series, while seeded inharmonicity offsets upper partials; held frequencies glide and ultrasonic partials fade out. Spatial turns distribute the whole bank once around the speakers at 1, squeeze it into an arc below 1, or wrap it repeatedly above 1. Across speakers offers rotation by dragging; One speaker replaces the colored partial field with one highlighted destination selected by click or drag. Optional clockwise, counterclockwise, and opposite-direction motion follows BPM and beats per turn while Play is active; motion starts off. The Shape-style panel places presets, Play/Pause, tempo, note/chord selection and chromatic keys on the right. On mobile, the graphic stays fixed while the fingerprint and parameter controls share one scroll pane; landscape places that pane beside the graphic. The current master knob, next-instrument link, square Audio button and MIDI-in-Settings presentation preserve the existing native controls. Cascade delay and initial wait use exact millisecond entry with 1 ms steps. An accelerating/slowing curve and seeded variation reshape the gaps while preserving total span; the timing strip previews scheduled entrances, including silent partial slots. Restart cascade briefly fades only the held note or chord before replaying it with the new timing, leaving other notes and transport intact. An eight-voice sample-clock engine renders the numbered discrete patch when it fits, with a labeled left/right stereo fallback and silent LFE. Artwork is generated from the instrument's canvas. Routing follows the [Web Audio channel contracts](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio-1.0/); physical channel assignments and spatial feel still need a hardware listening pass.
