# Witches Brew

Witches Brew is one continuous animated instrument. Her cauldron voice and
Gesticules fingers keep playing together, with different foreground balances in
the presets. The scene starts still and silent. A real click in the picture
arms Audio and fades in the performance. On the ground, pulling her hair firmly backward fades the
ensemble over 3.2 seconds, then holds the scene still. Stirring wakes only her
voice; bending her fingers wakes only the hand sounds. Each stays active, so
you can bring the two parts back separately. The same gestures work from the
initial quiet scene. Plucking the web starts only its own sound, leaving the
voice, fingers and automatic background sounds asleep. The mushrooms and floor
die restore the complete preset performance.
In flight, hair pulls reshape the shared delay while the ensemble keeps playing. The full-width picture contains the controls: mushrooms cycle presets,
the floor die randomizes settings, and grounded hair pulling hushes the scene. There is no preset
menu, playback/reset overlay, help button or parameter sidebar.
The cauldron sits over flickering flames, charred logs and rising embers.
Brightness remains on the **+ / −** keys.

| Gesture | Picture and sound |
| --- | --- |
| Stir faster, wider, or backward | The vortex and syllables follow speed, vowel opening and direction. |
| Tap her left, spoon-holding hand (or O) | Cycles slow, medium and fast stirring, then reverses and cycles those speeds in the other direction. Resumes automatic stirring and wakes only the voice if the scene was quiet. Works on the ground and in flight. |
| Lift the spoon | Seven colored motes choose mutter, whisper, creak, cackle, singing, moon wail or hex chatter. |
| Click her hat (or K) | Starts as the original intact hat. The first click leaves it intact, the second breaks it into burned scraps, and the third tumbles it into the cauldron. It stays gone until a new preset, ingredient spell or dice roll; moon refills do not restore it. Dragging does not count as a click. |
| Click her mouth | Advances to the next singing phrase and holds that character. |
| Pull her nose or hat | Nose length and vocal pitch/resonance change; both hat shapes stretch and change vocal texture, then relax when released. Nose reach is doubled and participates in presets and dice. |
| Pull her hair | Longer two-dimensional tugs stretch and curl the tangles. On the ground, a firm backward pull fades the complete mix over 3.2 seconds before resting; manual stirring fades in only her voice, while bending a finger wakes only the hand sounds. Bringing in one leaves the other asleep until its own gesture. In flight, pulling and circling change delay time, pitch travel, echo history and feedback, with no hush. |
| Click her eyes | Orange darting eyes accompany a short electrical processing accent and jagged picture distortion. |
| Tap the bloody face in the mist (or J) | Hiccup Head becomes solid and rushes forward with a scream, fills the picture, then vanishes. It stays out of the mist for six seconds afterward. Dragging the face still changes the ghost beat; a cancelled drag never triggers the scare. |
| Touch her palm or bend a finger | The actual elongated Gesticules hand features its instrumental voices. An articulated elbow meets the wrist at the forearm angle. |
| Click her forearm sleeve | Resumes automatic finger motion and cycles through the sixteen Gesticules hand animations while preserving the sound bank, tempo and flight/stirring mode. |
| Click a shelf instrument | Its short sound bite plays while it flies into the pot; musical parameters and scene colors fade toward its preset during the flight. |
| Click a tree visitor | BLECH MIDI, Syrinx or Creaturazoid plays from its branch for 3.2 seconds, then dives into the pot. |
| Grab the flying fabric | The green-and-pink Fabric Filter fills the flying background. Grab an exposed patch, stretch it, then release to ripple the cloth and filter its separate wind sound. The witch, moon, pot, mushrooms and hair noodles keep priority. The wind can play alone without waking the singer, fingers or background performers. |
| Click or drag a branch | Click to play its native L-system sound bite, with a pulse travelling through the branches. Mushrooms and the die choose new tree settings. Drag outward to widen the branches, inward to close them, or up/down for asymmetric curling. The excerpt bends in pitch and stereo position with the tree. |
| Touch the spider or web | The actual Spider Synth says “Nooooo! Bad witch!” as it dives into the potion, returning with the other ingredients. The seven web strings remain separately playable in a menacing scale, with a clear plucked attack and creaky tension. They can wake on their own without starting the voice, fingers or background performers. |
| Click the broom or moon | Toggles flight. The moon also refills all shelf and tree ingredients. Both arms, hands, trailing legs, broom and wild hair travel with the witch. |
| Pull and coil her flying hair noodles | Grab either red-and-cream strand and whip it into a new shape. Bring it near her hair for a small coil, or pull outward to fill half or all of the sky; release leaves a brief snaking recoil. Horizontal pulls change echo length, vertical pulls change pitch travel, spreading the coil reaches further into echo history, and winding circles raises or lowers feedback. |
| Click the right mushrooms | Chooses the next complete settings preset with a bubbling gloop and refills the ingredients, preserving whether she is flying or stirring. |
| Click the twenty-sided die | A ringing charm accompanies full-state randomization of every musical and visual setting. Refills ingredients and preserves flight/stirring. The die stays beside the pot in both modes. |
| Catch the cat, mouse or lizard | The visitor dives into the potion with a short sound. The cat meows as it goes. On the third successful cat capture, it emerges as a sparkling Quadruped unicorn and gallops away. |

The left cupboard has six shelves with three real instrument icons on each:
Alien Larynx, Hyper Rubix (4D), Digestazoid, Hybrinx, Roach Synth, FM Drums,
Rubix Cube Sequencer, Hiccup Head, Gesticules, Rattlesnake, Throatazoid,
Julie Saw, Puggler, Fractal Synthesis, Synthesaurus, Jaw Harp, Harmonicazoid
and Dentaphone. Five real instrument icons sit on the cupboard top: Quadruped,
Monstroid, Candy Coil Delay, SIMD Chiptune and SIMD 303.
BLECH MIDI, Syrinx and Creaturazoid occupy separate branches, with Creaturazoid
on an upper branch. Hybrinx appears once.
The full-height tree uses the L-system Delay instrument's seven generation colors,
with the moon behind its branches. The three visitors use enlarged portraits
and remain seated directly on the branches.
Enlarged transparent red BLECH logos sometimes pass through the flying sky,
playing an excerpt from its bundled Black MIDI demo.
Spider Synth's bulky King Baboon tarantula lives in a broad top-right corner web,
with radial spokes and bowed silk threads in front of the tree.
The model is based on [King Baboon Tarantula](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/king-baboon-tarantula-92c84c9dac9043ee81e87173e2ee0381)
by [Auckland Museum](https://sketchfab.com/aucklandmuseum), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Morphazoid normalized,
rigged and compressed the scan; [asset notes](../assets/spider-synth/skins/ASSET.md)
describe the modifications. Its placement in this web is artistic.

Ingredient sounds are bounded excerpts of the actual instruments' sound engines
and authored sources. They include fast Rubix Acid rhythms, cascading Rattlesnake
notes, SIMD chip and acid phrases, and Puggler’s fast punk guitar, drums and Oi.
Every Morphazoid ingredient has three sounds, cycling on each accepted click.
The next sound survives refills and preset changes; consumed icons still wait
for the next refill. BLECH's tree and flying appearances use dense passages from
Lightstorm, Seven Red Suns and A Soul Of Steel, with separate cycling for the
flying visitors.

Roach Synth combines its percussion with fluttering wings. These bites sit within
the ongoing mix; they do not instantiate each instrument's complete interface. See
[Sound bite sources](witch-sound-bites.md) for generation and provenance.
Consumed icons return when you click the mushrooms, moon or floor die, recall
a preset with W, or reset with Backspace. Successive ingredient clicks preserve
previously used icons. A new
click can redirect an ongoing preset transition; direct musical gestures take
over from the transition without resetting the audio clocks or output level.

The witch's two hands use Gesticules' weighted 3D hand model, sixteen authored
animations and ten instrumental source banks. Voice and fingers retain separate
clocks. Presets choose their balance, motion, speed, sound, vocal anatomy,
ghost beat, flight-path settings, color, distortion, tree shape, branch spread and curl. Presets,
ingredient transitions and the floor die preserve the live flight/stirring mode;
only the performer launches or lands her. Each preset has its own flight speed,
independent of stirring: **Bells beneath the full moon** and **Skimming the low fog**
drift slowly, while **Down the candy coil** and **A broom through the blood moon**
swoop quickly. Changing the pace continues from her current position.
The singer/finger balance varies
through presets and full randomization. Older saved presets retain their chosen voice
and original balance; new fields receive safe defaults.

Six spells leave one gesture to the player. **A secret in the steam**, **Older
than the door**, and **One last spell before dawn** wait for manual stirring;
the ladle coasts to rest when released. **At her fingertips**, **Five crooked
little instruments**, and **Bells from a drowned room** hold still fingers that
you can bend and leave in place. Singing and the ensemble continue in either
case, on the ground or in flight. Most presets retain both automatic motions;
each die roll has a small independent chance of choosing either manual setting.

Most factory and ingredient spells keep the picture steady while the witch
performs. Dice choose a steady picture 60% of the time, gentle warps 25%, and
glitch modes 15%. All eight looks remain available through presets and the die. VHS, static,
pixel and angular shifts have half their earlier horizontal displacement;
Pixel rot uses finer blocks so the scene stays readable.

Flight keeps the moon, clouds and cauldron while the cupboard, tree,
ghosts and animal visitors fade into the sky. The mushrooms remain playable. The witch remains
30% larger than her earlier flight form, and her spoon can still reach the pot.
A longer sweeping route carries smaller returning loops; twisting the hair
deepens those loops. Denser hair noodles cross the sky and bend the shared delay.
Occasional BLECH MIDI icons fly past, singly or in pairs, carrying their native
five-second phrase through the same flight delay. They leave the shelf intact.
The physical broom is wood and bundled bristles. Four screen-filling delay
strands grow directly from her hair; the flying fabric uses Fabric Filter’s
green columns and pink rows. Clicking the moon or broom again lands her without
stopping her song. Flight processing keeps the ensemble at a comparable level
to stirring while adding the shared delay. Fabric Filter shapes only its own wind
channel, mixed alongside the delayed performers; wind does not enter the delay.
Keyboard ingredient shortcuts remain available during flight.

The black cat is 20% larger than its preceding appearance and keeps its
Quadruped gait with louder foot contacts. It crosses every thirty seconds of Play;
a skeletal mouse and a lizard make staggered visits using their own Quadruped anatomy
and native foot-contact sounds, synchronized to their visible steps.
The mouse uses Quadruped's actual Skeleton skin, with articulated ribs, skull,
legs and tail bones.
The lizard crawls upside down across the top edge. The mouse follows the left
edge and bottom, reversing direction on alternate visits to climb up or down.
Capturing a visitor hides it for the rest of that visit. It can return on a later
visit. Stop freezes the visitor clock; changing presets does not restart it.
The third cat capture uses Quadruped's unicorn anatomy, gallop and Sparkle call:
it reaches the potion, leaps out with a glittering coat, and runs off the edge.
Only successful captures count; repeated clicks during a dive do not. Presets
keep the tally, while Backspace or a page reload starts it over.
Ghost portraits keep a background beat while grounded, with a more present
Creaturazoid voice that remains behind the singer and fingers. They can still be
raised or lowered to adjust the beat’s tempo.
The larger Hiccup Head ghost retains the bloody red Zombie Zoid skin. Its
transparent portrait at `assets/witch/hiccup-head-bloody.webp` is captured from
Hiccup Head's native `drawFace` renderer with `wild-ink` selected and beat zero
of its original `wild-ink-decay-fields.webp` atlas. Until tapped, it remains behind
the witch and fades into the mist with the other ghosts; the shelf miniature keeps its
usual catalogue portrait. See the [skin provenance](https://github.com/blechdom/morphazoid/blob/main/assets/hiccup-head/skins/README.md).
Its brief scream uses Hiccup Head's holler pressure and formant settings in a
compact synthesized voice. The foreground portrait follows that voice's clock;
reduced motion shows a stationary face instead of the rush.
Regenerate the portrait with `scripts/render-witch-hiccup-ghost.mjs`, pointing
`MORPHAZOID_QA_BASE_URL` at a running source preview.

With the picture focused: **A** cycles hand animations, **O** changes stirring pace/direction, **H** features fingers, **M** toggles flight, **B** lands,
**W** chooses the next whole spell, **D** rolls the die, **F** catches wind in the flying fabric, **V** changes her phrase, **X** shocks her eyes,
**T** plays the tree; hold **T** with arrow keys to spread or curl branches. **S** wakes the spider and **P** plucks the next web string. **J** calls the bloody face forward with a scream.
**C/N/L** catch the cat, mouse or lizard when present. Hold **1–5** and use the
arrows for individual fingers. **Q/E/R** add Gesticules, Rattlesnake or Throatazoid.
**[ / ]** select an ingredient and **Enter** sends it into the pot.
**G / Shift+G** adjusts the ghost tempo. **Backspace** resets the brew;
**Space** wakes/hushes; **Escape** hushes and releases.

The singer uses a fictional voiced/noisy source with vowel-like resonances;
backward means traversing the authored syllable path in reverse. It is not
recorded speech or a measured human-voice model. The independent fingers remain
instrumental. The owner-requested picture-wake gesture arms Audio directly;
passive loading and synthetic picture events stay silent. The masthead Audio
control can also wake or mute the performance. All effects have
bounded output, voice counts and release paths; automated checks establish
mechanical behavior, while listening and physical touchscreen feel require a
human pass.
