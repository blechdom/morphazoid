How it works / listening exercise

Try this first

Choose Soft agreement, enable Audio, and Play. Wait a few seconds, nudge Body 1, then listen as it returns to the pattern. Try No manners for slipping phases.

What you hear

A short high tick is the external drive. Three lower tones sound when their own orbiting beads cross the top of their rings.

What the picture means

Filled beads are the three body phases. Hollow markers show possible drive-aligned positions for each ratio. The central pulse is the drive. IN STEP means measured relative phase motion has settled; it does not mean all three bodies strike simultaneously.

The mechanism

The bodies prefer one revolution per 2, 3, and 4 drive beats. Those target ratios are chosen, but note times come from continuously evolving phases. Coupling pulls them toward the drive; detuning pulls them away. They share a drive, not direct connections to one another.

Controls

Drive tempo
External drive rate. It is not the note rate of all three bodies.
Pull into step
How strongly the drive pulls each body toward a stable phase relationship. Zero releases the drive; the bodies still move.
Restlessness
Signed natural-rate offset shared by all bodies. Positive runs faster; negative runs slower. Radial drags add an individual offset.
Body decay
Decay time of the three body tones, not the rate of motion.
Hear the drive
Volume of the high drive tick. Turning it down does not stop the drive.

Pause, reset, and memory

Presets retain body phases and individual detuning. Reset controls restores the three original phases and offsets but keeps Play and Audio unchanged.

Audio Off is mute, not Pause. It closes owned microphone tracks but retains transport. Monitor can pass live microphone audio while paused until you turn it off.

Keyboard

Focus the stage. Left/right selects a body; Enter or 1–3 nudges it. Up/down changes its detuning. Space toggles Play.

What this demo does not do

Three fixed bodies, no recordings or graph. This is classical phase locking, not a physical or quantum time crystal. Ratios are not automatically discovered.

About this work-in-progress instrument

A classical phase-locking experiment—not a simulation of a quantum time crystal.

Presets change parameters, not capabilities. Reset controls keeps recordings and learned routes. Demo material is synthesized locally.
