Oculoid

A playable eye. Use the labelled controls or keyboard if the canvas is unavailable.

Drag any iris to steer shared gaze, a lid to move it, or the background to turn the whole eye. Dress-up packs one to twelve eyeballs into the sheath; beyond four they stack at different depths. Banter, Harmony and Rock out give each eye its own animated and sounding part when Motion plays. Lid speed sets an independent rhythm relative to Eye tempo. Flash aims a beam; Poke presses the touched globe with adjustable diameter and depth; Stretch pulls a patch on the touched globe. These three tools are mutually exclusive. Retract lids holds both fully open; Hide lids shows the eyeballs alone. Tear drops once, Cry repeats every four beats at its own Cry tempo. On the focused canvas, arrows steer gaze or aim a selected tool. In Poke hold P; in Stretch, Enter grips or releases and arrows move the grip. Escape releases it. Hold L to blink when lids are present and not retracted. Audio must be explicitly enabled to hear gestures.

Eye model & instructions

Original textured globe and iris: DEGUIDER’s Realistic Human Eye, CC BY 4.0. Aviator sunglasses: Kimppo, CC BY 4.0. Morphazoid adds a spherical shell, movable lids, lashes, tears, vessels, pupil and sound; these are artistic approximations, not medical anatomy.

Drag the iris, gaze X/Y pad or lids to play; drag outside the eye to turn the entire rig. The black loading-eye look is a selectable 3D appearance that rotates and deforms with the globe. Retract lids holds them open; Hide lids leaves the eyeball alone. Tear releases one drop from the lid corner, while Cry repeats every four beats at its own tempo even without Motion. Stretch tugs locally without rotating the eyeball. Poke has independent diameter and depth knobs; a closed lid still blocks all surface tools. Audio starts off; movements and drops make short sounds, not drones. Model, license & controls.
