Evidence and limitswhat is measured versus assumed

The file/plectrum mechanism, roughly 4.8 kHz calling carrier, and a pushed-wing Q of 23.4 come from a 2003 Teleogryllus oceanicus study. The 7.9 Q starting control comes from 2025 live-wing vibrometry and finite-element work; its measurements and models are on Dryad.

A single microphone can fit carrier and closing-stroke timing. It cannot uniquely recover tooth contacts, damping, left/right coupling, material, or anatomy. Those controls are an inspectable physical hypothesis, not a claim about the recorded animal.
