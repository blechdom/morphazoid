Model topology Pressure, two reeds, nonlinear player load

Every chamber owns a blow reed and a draw reed. Signed pressure selects the primary tongue; the opposing tongue joins during bends while the moving vocal tract feeds a nonlinear acoustic load back into both reeds.

Hold the upper half of a numbered chamber to blow its upper reed, or hold the lower half to draw its lower reed. Move across the ten chambers while holding to glissando. Drag HOLE / MOUTH horizontally to select the anchor chamber and vertically to change the mouth aperture between a one-hole single note, two-hole double-stop, and three- or four-hole chord. Drag AIR horizontally for logarithmic automatic breath speed and vertically for pressure. Drag BEND horizontally for paired-reed bend and vertically for reed gap. Drag BLUES horizontally for technique rate and vertically for amount. Drag TONGUE in two dimensions, and drag CUP horizontally to open or close the cover-hand filter and vertically for growl. Drag LIPS inward or outward, and drag TRACT horizontally for throat opening and vertically for vocal feedback. The Breath rhythm selector drives patterned draw and blow attacks while Auto is on. Equivalent labeled controls follow the diagram. Number keys one through nine and zero select holes. Left bracket holds Draw, right bracket holds Blow, and Space holds Blow while the canvas is focused.
