What is physical here?

four sources · one tract

Mammal calls drive a nonlinear two-mass larynx. Bird calls drive independently detunable paired syringeal labia. Frogs use pressure-driven membranes with sac/head modes, and mouse gestures use an impinging-jet whistle mapped into the audible range.

The body is persistent and absolute. Its size, roundness, source tissue, throat, cavity, and outlet shape every one of the 50 voices and body gestures. Pads and rhythm presets replace only the gesture; they never load another creature.

Body percussion is still physical. Hisses use unvoiced airway turbulence; hooves, feet, jaws, claws, tongues, tails, and feather vanes excite deterministic body and cavity modes inside the same monophonic worklet. No recordings are used.

Native motion remains articulated. Multi-peak pressure, pitch, closure, mouth, cavity, roughness, split, and balance contours survive inside the selected body. Each onset also restarts call-local Speed and Depth envelopes, so modulation can accelerate, widen, settle, and surge across one sound.

The creature is monophonic. Every pad and sequencer column targets one persistent AudioWorklet and one travelling-wave airway. The short rhythmic attack makes each occupied step speak promptly while later pulses and long releases keep their authored shape.

Sequence presets are complete grooves. Each one recalls its own length, tempo, and swing. The dance patterns tile compact sixteen-step creature-percussion motifs; moving any timing control marks the rhythm Custom, while the spacious patterns leave long calls room to unfold.

Visible anatomy performs. Voices inflate the belly and lungs, telescope the neck, and throw open the mouth. Stomps drop the forelegs into impact rings; footsteps alternate hooves; claws rake sideways; the tail whips; feathers ruffle independently; panting pumps the lungs; lapping throws the tongue; and crunching snaps the jaw and teeth. Every hit pulses the palette.

Output dynamics are controlled. A fast soft-knee compressor catches the most explosive cross-species peaks while preserving velocity accents and quiet micro-calls.
