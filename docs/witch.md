# Witches Brew

Witches Brew is one continuous animated instrument. Her cauldron voice and
Gesticules fingers keep playing together, with different foreground balances in
the presets. The scene starts still and silent. A real click in the picture
arms Audio and fades in the performance. On the ground, pulling her hair firmly backward fades the
ensemble over 3.2 seconds, then holds the scene still in manual mode. Stirring
and face gestures play only her voice; held fingers play only those digits.
They release when the gesture ends. Web plucks, tree phrases, ingredients and
animal captures play independently without restoring automatic loops. Ingredient
transitions still change the scene's settings, while manual mode stays active.
The mushrooms and floor SHAPES charm restore the complete preset performance.
Before the first hair hush, voice and hand gestures can wake their preset's
automatic parts separately from the initial quiet scene.
In flight, hair pulls reshape the shared delay while the ensemble keeps playing. The full-width picture contains the controls: mushrooms cycle presets,
the floor SHAPES charm randomizes settings, and grounded hair pulling hushes the scene. There is no preset
menu, playback/reset overlay, help button or parameter sidebar.
The cauldron sits over flickering flames, charred logs and rising embers.
Brightness remains on the **+ / −** keys.

| Gesture | Picture and sound |
| --- | --- |
| Stir faster, wider, or backward | The vortex and syllables follow speed, vowel opening and direction. |
| Tap her left, spoon-holding hand (or O) | Cycles slow, medium and fast stirring, then reverses and cycles those speeds in the other direction. After a hair hush it auditions a short voice phrase without restarting a loop; otherwise it resumes that part's automatic stirring. Works on the ground and in flight. |
| Lift the spoon | Seven colored motes choose mutter, whisper, creak, cackle, singing, moon wail or hex chatter. |
| Click her hat (or K) | Starts as the original intact hat. The first click leaves it intact, the second breaks it into burned scraps, and the third tumbles it into the cauldron. It stays gone until a new preset, ingredient spell or shape click; moon refills do not restore it. Dragging does not count as a click. |
| Click her mouth | Advances to the next singing phrase and holds that character. |
| Pull her nose or hat | Nose length and vocal pitch/resonance change; both hat shapes stretch and change vocal texture, then relax when released. Nose reach is doubled and participates in presets and randomization. |
| Pull her hair | Longer two-dimensional tugs stretch and curl the tangles. On the ground, a firm backward pull fades the complete mix over 3.2 seconds before resting; manual stirring fades in only her voice, while bending a finger wakes only the hand sounds. Bringing in one leaves the other asleep until its own gesture. In flight, pulling and circling change delay time, pitch travel, echo history and feedback, with no hush. |
| Click her eyes | Orange darting eyes accompany a short electrical processing accent and jagged picture distortion. |
| Tap any bloody face in the mist (or J) | Hiccup Head becomes solid and rushes forward with a scream, fills the picture, then vanishes. The lurking faces return as soon as the scream ends. Each visit can bring a different scream, length and level. Dragging a face still changes the ghost beat; a cancelled drag never triggers the scare. |
| Touch her palm or bend a finger | The actual elongated Gesticules hand features its instrumental voices. An articulated elbow meets the wrist at the forearm angle. |
| Click her forearm sleeve | Resumes automatic finger motion and cycles through the sixteen Gesticules hand animations while preserving the sound bank, tempo and flight/stirring mode. |
| Click a shelf instrument | Its short sound bite plays while it flies into the pot; musical parameters and scene colors fade toward its preset during the flight. |
| Click a tree visitor | BLECH MIDI, Syrinx or Creaturazoid plays from its branch for 3.2 seconds, then dives into the pot. |
| Catch a flying BLECH MIDI logo (or I while one passes) | Its Rin-W Black MIDI pattern accelerates into a dense, layered five-second loop, then releases into the flight delay. The caught logo shudders; repeated clicks renew one burst rather than piling up voices. It can play alone after a hush. |
| Grab the flying fabric | The green-and-pink Fabric Filter fills the flying background. Grab an exposed patch, stretch it, then release to ripple the cloth and filter its separate wind sound. The witch, moon, pot, mushrooms and hair noodles keep priority. The wind can play alone without waking the singer, fingers or background performers. |
| Click or drag a branch | Click to play one L-system PM phrase with a pulse travelling through the branches. Drag outward to widen the turn angle, inward to close it, or up/down for asymmetric curling. Those changes alter the live modulation and stereo image; a held drag never restarts the phrase. Mushrooms and the SHAPES charm choose new tree settings. |
| Touch the spider or web | The actual Spider Synth cycles “NOOOOOOOOO !!!!!”, “SPIIIIIIDER!!!!!!” and “I love you mom and dad!” as it dives into the potion, returning with the other ingredients. Tap for one sharp, creaky pluck; sweep or scrub across the silk for successive plucks, including multiple rings in the same note area. Fast swipes follow the crossed threads through seven menacing pitches. The web can wake on its own without starting the voice, fingers or background performers. |
| Click the broom or Oculoid moon | Toggles flight. The moon also refills all shelf and tree ingredients. Both arms, hands, trailing legs, broom and wild hair travel with the witch. |
| Pull and coil her flying hair noodles | Grab either red-and-cream strand and whip it into a new shape. Bring it near her hair for a small coil, or pull outward to fill half or all of the sky; release leaves a brief snaking recoil. Horizontal pulls change echo length, vertical pulls change pitch travel, spreading the coil reaches further into echo history, and winding circles raises or lowers feedback. |
| Click the right mushrooms | The glowing 3D wireframe mushrooms choose the next complete settings preset with a bubbling gloop and refill the ingredients, preserving whether she is flying or stirring. |
| Click the SHAPES charm | A native SHAPES form morphs into the next 3D/4D form and plays a short geometry-derived sound while randomizing every musical and visual setting. Refills ingredients and preserves flight/stirring and output level. The charm stays beside the pot in both modes; rapid clicks smoothly retarget it. |
| Catch the cat, mouse or lizard | The visitor dives into the potion with a short sound. The cat meows as it goes. On the third successful cat capture, it emerges as a sparkling Quadruped unicorn and gallops away. |

The floor charm alternates native SHAPES forms: tesseract, cube, hyperpyramid,
octahedron, Klein bottle, prism, star hyperprism and torus. Each click morphs
over 0.9 seconds and plays a 1.4-second plane or hyperplane scan through the
selected form, using SHAPES' geometry-to-pitch and FM/PM synthesis mappings.
Its sound joins the ensemble's flight delay. Reduced motion uses a stationary
crossfade between forms.

The left cupboard has six shelves with three real instrument icons on each:
Alien Larynx, Hyper Rubix (4D), Digestazoid, Hybrinx, Roach Synth, FM Drums,
Rubix Cube Sequencer, Hiccup Head, Gesticules, Rattlesnake, SIMD Chiptune,
Julie Saw, Puggler, Fractal Synthesis, Synthesaurus, Jaw Harp, Harmonicazoid
and Dentaphone. Four real instrument icons sit on the cupboard top: Quadruped,
Monstroid, Candy Coil Delay and SIMD 303.
BLECH MIDI, Syrinx and Creaturazoid occupy separate branches, with Creaturazoid
on an upper branch. Hybrinx appears once.
The full-height tree uses uniform branch thickness and the L-system Delay
instrument's seven generation colors. Each click plays one PM note for every
visible branch, with a higher 294 Hz starting note. Sound and playheads follow
the same native branch distances and forks over one 2.05-second traversal,
followed by short release tails. Each branch has a sustained ADSR envelope
adapted to its length. Branch spread changes its turn angle and
modulation index from 3 to 8; curl changes asymmetry, modulator ratio and stereo
position. Live edits do not restart the phrase, and clicks during a phrase do
not queue another one. Changing the tree shape releases the old phrase; click
the new tree to play its own branches.

Behind its branches, [Oculoid](../oculoid.html)'s actual lidless eye globe turns
in three dimensions with the ladle. It sits a little farther right, with no
moon disc or glow behind it; its click target follows its small stirring sway.
Quiet and reduced-motion states hold its pose. A small transparent snapshot
renderer uses a texture-reduced copy of the native model, with no extra animation
loop. The previous lidless portrait is a loading fallback.
The eye model is DEGUIDER's
[Realistic Human Eye](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/realistic-human-eye-b68ae822b45443edb812950993ece362),
licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with Oculoid's
authored iris color and lighting; [source and modifications](../assets/oculoid/SOURCE.LICENSE.txt).
The three visitors use enlarged portraits
and remain seated directly on the branches.
Enlarged transparent red BLECH logos sometimes pass through the flying sky,
looping dense patterns from its bundled Rin-W Black MIDI collection.
Spider Synth's bulky King Baboon tarantula lives in a broad top-right corner web,
with radial spokes and bowed silk threads in front of the tree.
The model is based on [King Baboon Tarantula](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/king-baboon-tarantula-92c84c9dac9043ee81e87173e2ee0381)
by [Auckland Museum](https://sketchfab.com/aucklandmuseum), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Morphazoid normalized,
rigged and compressed the scan; [asset notes](../assets/spider-synth/skins/ASSET.md)
describe the modifications. Its placement in this web is artistic.

Ingredient sounds are bounded excerpts of the actual instruments' sound engines
and authored sources. They include fast Rubix Acid rhythms, cascading Rattlesnake
notes, SIMD chip and acid phrases, and Puggler’s fast punk guitar, drums and Oi.
Every Morphazoid ingredient has three sounds, cycling on each accepted click.
The next sound survives refills and preset changes; consumed icons still wait
for the next refill. BLECH's tree and flying appearances use Rin-W's
3-Beat Funnel 128, 4-Beat Funnel 88 and Webs Volcanic, with separate cycling for
the flying visitors. Flying patterns loop through each pass and sit louder in
the mix; they retain their short fade when the witch lands or rests.

Roach Synth combines its percussion with fluttering wings. These bites sit within
the ongoing mix; they do not instantiate each instrument's complete interface. See
[Sound bite sources](witch-sound-bites.md) for generation and provenance.
Consumed icons return when you click the mushrooms, moon or floor SHAPES charm, recall
a preset with W, or reset with Backspace. Successive ingredient clicks preserve
previously used icons. A new
click can redirect an ongoing preset transition; direct musical gestures take
over from the transition without resetting the audio clocks or output level.

The witch's two hands use Gesticules' weighted 3D hand model, sixteen authored
animations and ten instrumental source banks. Voice and fingers retain separate
clocks. Presets choose their balance, motion, speed, sound, vocal anatomy,
ghost beat, flight-path settings, color, distortion, tree shape, branch spread and curl. Presets,
ingredient transitions and the floor SHAPES charm preserve the live flight/stirring mode;
only the performer launches or lands her. Each preset has its own flight speed,
independent of stirring: **Bells beneath the full moon** and **Skimming the low fog**
drift slowly, while **Down the candy coil** and **A broom through the blood moon**
swoop quickly. Changing the pace continues from her current position.
The singer/finger balance varies
through presets and full randomization. Older saved presets retain their chosen voice
and original balance; new fields receive safe defaults.

Six spells leave one gesture to the player. **A secret in the steam**, **Older
than the door**, and **One last spell before dawn** wait for manual stirring;
the ladle coasts to rest when released. **At her fingertips**, **Five crooked
little instruments**, and **Bells from a drowned room** hold still fingers that
you can bend and leave in place. Singing and the ensemble continue in either
case, on the ground or in flight. Most presets retain both automatic motions;
each shape click has a small independent chance of choosing either manual setting.

Most factory and ingredient spells keep the picture steady while the witch
performs. Dice choose a steady picture 60% of the time, gentle warps 25%, and
glitch modes 15%. All eight looks remain available through presets and the shape charm. VHS, static,
pixel and angular shifts have half their earlier horizontal displacement;
Pixel rot uses finer blocks so the scene stays readable.

Flight keeps the moon, clouds and cauldron while the cupboard, tree,
ghosts and animal visitors fade into the sky. The cat, mouse, lizard and escaping
unicorn fall silent at takeoff; their footsteps and capture calls stay out of
the flight delay. Their visit clocks continue, so landing resumes the current
visit without replaying missed steps. The mushrooms remain playable. The witch remains
30% larger than her earlier flight form, and her spoon can still reach the pot.
A longer sweeping route carries smaller returning loops; twisting the hair
deepens those loops. Denser hair noodles cross the sky and bend the shared delay.
Occasional BLECH MIDI icons fly past, singly or in pairs, carrying their native
five-second phrase through the same flight delay. They leave the shelf intact.
The physical broom is wood and bundled bristles. Four screen-filling delay
strands grow directly from her hair; the flying fabric uses Fabric Filter’s
green columns and pink rows. Clicking the moon or broom again lands her without
stopping her song. Flight processing keeps the ensemble at a comparable level
to stirring while adding the shared delay. Fabric Filter shapes only its own wind
channel, mixed alongside the delayed performers; wind does not enter the delay.
Keyboard ingredient shortcuts remain available during flight.

The black cat is 20% larger than its preceding appearance and keeps its
Quadruped gait with louder foot contacts. It crosses every thirty seconds of Play;
a skeletal mouse and a lizard make staggered visits using their own Quadruped anatomy
and native foot-contact sounds, synchronized to their visible steps.
The mouse uses Quadruped's actual Skeleton skin, with articulated ribs, skull,
legs and tail bones.
The lizard crawls upside down across the top edge. The mouse follows the left
edge and bottom, reversing direction on alternate visits to climb up or down.
Capturing a visitor hides it for the rest of that visit. It can return on a later
visit. Stop freezes the visitor clock; changing presets does not restart it.
The third cat capture uses Quadruped's unicorn anatomy, gallop and Sparkle call:
it reaches the potion, leaps out with a glittering coat, and runs off the edge.
Only successful captures count; repeated clicks during a dive do not. Presets
keep the tally, while Backspace or a page reload starts it over.
Three copies of the bloody Hiccup Head face lurk behind the grounded witch.
They remain faintly visible between beats and every face can trigger the scare.
Their existing background beat, including Creaturazoid's voice, stays behind
the singer and fingers. Drag a face up or down to adjust that beat's tempo.
All three faces share the bloody red Zombie Zoid skin. Its
transparent portrait at `assets/witch/hiccup-head-bloody.webp` is captured from
Hiccup Head's native `drawFace` renderer with `wild-ink` selected and beat zero
of its original `wild-ink-decay-fields.webp` atlas. Until tapped, the faces remain behind
the witch and breathe in the mist; the shelf miniature keeps its
usual catalogue portrait. See the [skin provenance](https://github.com/blechdom/morphazoid/blob/main/assets/hiccup-head/skins/README.md).
The original scream, long wail, louder shriek and low rasp use Hiccup Head's
holler pressure and formant settings in one compact synthesized voice.
The first scare keeps the original sound; later visits choose a different variant
without repeating immediately. The foreground portrait follows that voice's clock;
reduced motion shows a stationary face instead of the rush.
Regenerate the portrait with `scripts/render-witch-hiccup-ghost.mjs`, pointing
`MORPHAZOID_QA_BASE_URL` at a running source preview.

With the picture focused: **A** cycles hand animations, **O** changes stirring pace/direction, **H** features fingers, **M** toggles flight, **B** lands,
**W** chooses the next whole spell, **D** morphs the SHAPES charm and randomizes the spell, **F** catches wind in the flying fabric, **V** changes her phrase, **X** shocks her eyes,
**T** plays the tree; hold **T** with arrow keys to spread or curl branches. **S** wakes the spider and **P** plucks the next web string. **J** calls the bloody face forward with a scream.
**C/N/L** catch the cat, mouse or lizard when present. Hold **1–5** and use the
arrows for individual fingers. **Q/E/R** add Gesticules, Rattlesnake or SIMD Chiptune.
**[ / ]** select an ingredient and **Enter** sends it into the pot.
**G / Shift+G** adjusts the ghost tempo. **Backspace** resets the brew;
**Space** wakes/hushes; **Escape** hushes and releases.

The singer uses a fictional voiced/noisy source with vowel-like resonances;
backward means traversing the authored syllable path in reverse. It is not
recorded speech or a measured human-voice model. The independent fingers remain
instrumental. The owner-requested picture-wake gesture arms Audio directly;
passive loading and synthetic picture events stay silent. The masthead Audio
control can also wake or mute the performance. All effects have
bounded output, voice counts and release paths; automated checks establish
mechanical behavior, while listening and physical touchscreen feel require a
human pass.

## Mobile rendering

Safari's missing Canvas 2D color filter is handled by a reusable GPU color pass.
It preserves the hue/saturation palette, including mushroom changes, without
reading pixels back every frame or recoloring the foreground Hiccup scare.
The pass follows resize and context restoration and releases its resources on
page teardown. Browsers with native canvas filters keep using them.

The quiet picture paints only when something changes, including an image finishing
loading. Playing uses a capped graphics clock independent of audio; hidden and
cached pages cancel their drawing and gesture timers. Canvas resolution adapts
to the visible stage and sustained rendering pressure without changing presets,
sound, geometry or touch coordinates. Static tree/web strokes and current hair
geometry are cached with bounded storage. Unchanged hand and spider poses reuse
their snapshots; diagnostics no longer render the spider.

Witch-owned [model derivatives](../assets/witch/README.md) retain the complete
native meshes, rigs and animations. Phones load 512-pixel hand textures, reducing
their decoded/uploaded pixel count by 93.75% and the hand download from 3.81 to
1.69 MB. Larger screens use 1024-pixel textures. The tarantula uses a 512-pixel
atlas. Audio skips only provably empty web, wind-filter and delay-history work;
336,000 compared reference frames, including releases and control changes,
matched the original samples exactly.

The October 26 optimization was compared with `9a35042b` in Chromium
151.0.7922.34, using 390×844/DPR 3, then 844×390, with 4× CPU throttling and
approximately four seconds per scene:

| Scene | Painted frames/second before → after | Mean paint time before → after |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Resting | 11.6 → 0 (no repeated work) | 58.9 → 0 ms |
| Brewing | 6.8 → 14.6 | 116.7 → 60.5 ms |
| Flying | 7.1 → 15.4 | 111.1 → 57.3 ms |
| Static distortion | 6.5 → 13.2 | 140.0 → 66.2 ms |
| Landscape distortion | 6.5 → 12.7 | 138.3 → 68.7 ms |

These are relative browser measurements, not physical-phone frame rates or a
Safari acceptance result. Automated touch checks cover portrait, landscape,
tablet resizing, independent source wake, hair hush, flight, cancellation,
asset-ready repaint and cached-page resume. Physical-device feel and listening
still need a human pass.

To repeat the profile against a running source preview, use its exact origin:

```sh
MORPHAZOID_QA_BASE_URL=http://localhost:3517 node scripts/qa/witch-mobile-profile.mjs
```

`WITCH_PROFILE_CPU=1` disables CPU throttling; `WITCH_PROFILE_OUTPUT` selects the
JSON artifact (default `/tmp/witch-mobile-profile.json`). Keep other benchmarks
idle during measurement. The script checks the served app/renderer bytes and
records frame times, long tasks, audio-clock progress, graphics budgets and
browser errors. It does not install quality controls in the instrument.
