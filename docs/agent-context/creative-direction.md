# Morphazoid's creative direction

These are **working patterns**, not a template for every instrument. They
summarize the existing product principles in `AGENTS.md` and the instrument
skills, checked against an October 2026 review of safely scoped personal
project conversations (28 deduplicated Codex root requests and one root
conversation each from Claude and Cursor). Mixed or uncertain histories and
unavailable machines were excluded; counts are not votes, and no transcripts
or session identifiers belong in this repository.

## Make the odd thing playable

- Start with a distinctive, legible mechanism: a performer action or model
  event visibly changes the object *and* audibly changes its sound. Build that
  causal loop before a long control panel, preset bank, or explanation.
- Keep direct dragging, holding, drawing, and meaningful motion in the main
  graphic. Give a performer a clear way to start, stop, recover, and play again.
  Visual decoration may be strange; false button affordances and ornamental
  DSP controls are not useful strangeness.
- Choose related gestures, image, and timbre; do not paste a generic synthesizer
  behind a novel drawing. Preserve existing musical identity on refactors.
- Let sound continue through non-transport edits when possible. Bounded
  randomization and presets should open expressive territory without trapping
  the performer or destroying a good live state.

## Style: expressive, spare, readable

- Favor dark surfaces, strong contrast, saturated accents, structural negative
  space, and compact native controls. Use borders only for real regions; avoid
  nested card chrome, passive tiles, and meters or engineering telemetry that
  displace the playing surface.
- Titles are part of the composition but still one coherent, accessible title.
  Verify legibility and reachability on desktop, phone portrait, and short
  landscape; move secondary controls rather than shrinking the central gesture.
- Evocative language is design evidence: translate a phrase into geometry,
  interaction, and audible behavior, not permanent marketing copy on the stage.
  Keep prose in an adjacent note when it is not needed during performance.
- Physics, animals, mathematics, and cultural sources can inspire the sound.
  Distinguish a sourced model from an artistic approximation; let curiosity
  survive without presenting speculative mappings as validated science.

## Musical and working preferences

- Prefer continuous, causal pitch relationships over automatic equal-tempered,
  Western-scale, or pentatonic quantization. An explicit optional tuning is
  different from forcing the whole instrument into a scale.
- Aim for a small playable vertical slice, then test a real route, live edits,
  representative extremes, mobile gestures, and audio continuity. Automated
  checks establish mechanics; listening and physical interaction still matter.
- Reuse existing primitives and two relevant siblings *after* checking their
  current contracts. Keep genuine differences between instruments rather than
  unifying their voices merely to simplify code.
