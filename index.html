Morphazoid

Morphazoid is a set of browser music instruments. It turns geometry, recursion, algorithms, small quantum simulations, microphone input, and audio files into sound.

01

Basic operation

Every tool has its own controls and audio state.

  1. Use the main menu to open an instrument. Audio starts off.
  2. Select Audio, Input, or the page's start control. The browser needs a click or tap before it can play sound.
  3. Set the output level before starting dense textures, feedback, or many voices.
  4. Start the page's playhead, reader, transport, or source. Many geometry pages let motion and audio run separately.
  5. Use Reset all parameters, Reset, or the page's reset control to return to the default settings.

Microphone pages ask for browser permission when input starts. Headphones help prevent feedback. File pages load the selected file in the browser and use it as a sound source.

02

Geometry Synths

These pages make sound from geometric contacts and intersections.

Shape

Plays a 2D contour with moving points, scan lines, or radial rays. Position, corner shape, and crossing angle shape pitch, level, pan, and timbre.

Lattice

Slides an editable isohedral tiling under a fixed reader line. Each edge crossing becomes a contact, so dense patterns can play several voices at once.

Spiral

Warps an isohedral tiling into log-polar space. Radius, angle, and spiral readers scan the warped edges and feed the selected synth mode.

Solid

Cuts a 3D wireframe with a movable plane. Rotation, stretch, skew, and plane position change the intersections and the sound.

Hyper

Cuts a 4D wireframe with a moving W hyperplane. XW, YW, and ZW rotations change which 4D edges cross the reader.

03

Physics Synths

Motion drives the score. Points, bodies, fields, and graphs move; their positions, contacts, forces, and energy make sound.

Gravity Walk

Moves a bead along a polygon, star, or circle. Height, speed, and corner impacts turn potential and kinetic energy into sound.

Ricochet

Bounces balls through editable arenas. Edge angle sets pitch, impact speed sets level, and rotating walls can add or remove energy.

Rigidity

Loads pinned bar-and-joint structures under gravity. Triangles, squares, and trusses show where constraints hold or fail.

Rolling Measure

Rolls regular polygons from vertex to vertex. Centroid lift and contact changes control turn angle, no-slip motion, and impact loss.

Falling Forms

Drops rigid shapes into a bounded stage. Impacts, rotation, support points, friction, and settling shape the sound.

Charge Garden

Places positive and negative charges in a softened field. Tracers and equipotential lines show how the sources combine.

Packing Pressure

Compresses falling disks into a contact network. Forces, density, coordination, and jams become visible and audible.

Geodesic Drift

Compares nearby straightest paths on a plane, cylinder, sphere, and torus. Path separation shows how each surface bends motion.

Kinetic Hull

Moves points and recomputes the convex hull and Delaunay edges. Boundary changes and edge flips trigger sound.

04

Geometry Drum Machines

These pages trigger a shared sixteen-voice drum bank from geometric events.

Shape Drum Machine

Turns contour contacts and corner events into drum hits. Position, incidence, phase, and corner shape choose and vary the voices.

Lattice Drum Machine

Turns lattice-reader contacts into drum hits. Edge class, orientation, height, and incidence choose or retune voices.

Spiral Drum Machine

Turns log-polar tiling contacts into drum hits. The reader follows the resulting radial and spiral geometry.

Solid Drum Machine

Turns plane-wireframe intersections into drum hits. Wireframe motion and plane angle shape the pattern.

Hyper Drum Machine

Turns 4D hyperplane intersections into drum hits. 4D position and rotation choose the active voices.

L-System Drum Machine

Turns recursive branch-head traversal into drum hits. Branch depth, turn angle, generation, phase, and position choose or retune the voices.

05

Signal & Voice

These pages reshape microphones, files, and recorded loops.

Lumber

Records loops as rings. Each ring has direction, level, pan, mute/solo, filters, pitch handles, playheads, and shared delay.

L-mic

Runs live mic input through recursive L-system branches. Branch timing makes delays; turns make pitch shifts; the tree shows both full and audible branches.

Graph Delay

Sends live mic audio through a generated delay graph. Topology, route switches, pitch shifts, and feedback shape the paths.

Throatazoid

Feeds mic input, an internal glottis, or both through one to seven vocal tracts. Throats, tongues, noses, closures, and gestures shape the voice.

06

Fractals & Recursion

These instruments use recursion to shape timing, pitch, structure, or DSP.

L-System

Expands turtle grammars into branching scores. Iteration, timing, turn angle, length taper, and playback mode shape the voice tree.

Recursion

Runs the Fuzzy Donut recursive DSP instrument. It can use noise, impulses, mic capture, or a file, then shows the process as a manifold, spectrum stack, or routing view.

Julia

Traces a Julia-set boundary and maps left/right turns to cyclic Shepard pitch. The self-similarity view finds related arcs and lets you hear them.

07

Algorithmic Sequencers

These pages sonify algorithm steps.

Sorting Algorithms

Runs Bubble, Insertion, Selection, Merge, and Quick Sort on the same shuffle. Comparisons, swaps, and writes become tones placed by index.

08

Barber Shop Poles

These pages create endless-sounding pitch or delay motion with staggered voices and read heads.

Shepard–Risset

Makes continuous rising or falling pitch. Octave Bank keeps the clean illusion; Morphisma Sweep restores the older sweep presets. Direction, speed, range, density, and stereo spread are adjustable.

Sandy Syrup Delay

Processes mic or file audio with staggered grains. Sand holds grain rates; Syrup moves them.

Striped Sludge Delay

Processes mic or file audio with staggered delay heads. Each head moves toward the source pitch at mid-sweep, then away.

Candy Coil Delay

Uses staggered variable delays for repeated rises or falls. Tap timing, ratio lock, head tilt, feedback, and mix set the motion.

09

Chaotic Synths

These pages make oscillator systems from recursion, nonlinear feedback, or fractal sums.

Recursive FM

Nests FM operators so each level drives the next. Depth, carrier frequency, offset, modulation amount, and divisor are adjustable.

Recursive PM

Folds a sine carrier through recursive phase operators. Deeper levels add smaller phase offsets, and depth changes crossfade.

Chaotic FM

Sends oscillators through a nonlinear FM cascade. A bounded tanh stage shapes each signal before it drives the next oscillator.

Chaotic PM

Shapes each recursive signal with tanh, then uses it for phase modulation. Smooth mode removes phase-wrap jumps. Legacy / Raw keeps the original experiment for comparison.

Weierstrass

Sums exponentially spaced oscillators. Wave mode plays the sum; FM and phase modes use the same bank as a modulation path.

10

Quantum Synths

These pages sonify small, exact classical simulations of quantum systems. They are demos, not quantum-hardware recordings.

Order Tones

Turns modular repetition into a loop, then applies an inverse-QFT model. Interference becomes a probability comb, and measurement shots try to recover the order and factors.

Bell Square

Simulates two atoms with a controlled collision phase. Measurement axes, probabilities, and repeated shots show correlations while each local result stays random.

Annealogue

Moves eight three-qubit states through an anneal. Energy, probability, and phase become a distorted cube and an eight-voice choir; a greedy classical path is shown for comparison.

11

Experiments (works-in-progress)

Small science and math sonification studies.

RISSET-MOIRE

Crosses rising and falling Shepard lattices. Line angles set glissando rates; paired layers and weighted crossings emphasize coincidences.

Chladni Plate

Draws standing-wave nodal curves on a square plate. The selected mode and strike point shape a small bank of partials.

Spring Choir

Simulates a coupled mass-spring chain. Motion projects onto normal modes that become a quiet oscillator choir.

Gear Ratio Drums

Rotates two meshed cogs at tooth-locked rates. Tooth contacts trigger short drum tones while the gear ratio supplies the polyrhythm.

Cellular Automata

Scrolls elementary automata rows. Live cells trigger a sparse pentatonic scan; density and transitions feed continuous tones.

Prime Sieve

Runs the sieve of Eratosthenes across a number grid. Prime discoveries become a sparse rising melody; composite eliminations become quiet ticks.

Lissajous Orbits

Combines perpendicular harmonic motion into closed curves. The same integer ratio controls both the visible lobes and a two-oscillator interval.

Pendulum Wave

Gives neighboring pendulums related periods that drift apart and reunite. Center crossings make a spatial melody; phase coherence feeds a drone.

Double Pendulum

Releases two double pendulums from nearly identical angles. Their divergence widens an interval as deterministic chaos becomes visible.

Reaction-Diffusion

Evolves a Gray-Scott chemical field through spots, bands, and splits. Reagent coverage and edge energy control a small sound field.

Atomic Orbitals

Draws signed planar slices of hydrogen orbitals. Quantum numbers and nodes organize the harmonic bank.

DNA Translator

Scans editable DNA through four base pitches and the codon table. Each completed triplet adds a tone for its amino acid.

Neural Pulse

Sends four inputs through a fixed weighted network. Layer crossings trigger sound; the demo does not train the network.

Fourier Epicycles

Chains rotating harmonic vectors into sine, square, triangle, and sawtooth waves. Every visible vector is also an audible partial.

Gravity Lens

Splits one source into two apparent images with an idealized lens. Image position, magnification, and path delay become a paired interval.

12

Instruments

Reusable instruments and analysis tools.

FM Drums

Edits the sixteen-voice drum bank used by the geometry drum machines. The voices use FM and shaped noise, and banks can be saved locally or copied as JSON.

Linear Drums

Maps a logarithmic frequency range onto one continuous percussion body. Drum and pitched-percussion engines add shaped pitch or strike-height curves, percussion envelopes, and sixteen preset fields without hard timbre boundaries.

Rattle Snake Boogie

Paints solid notes, freehand glissandi, ring outlines, and parameter fields onto a looping time-frequency canvas powered by the continuous percussion engine.

Sample Drums

Edits a matching sixteen-slot 808/909 sample bank. Samples load in the browser and can replace the FM engine in Lattice Drum Machine.

Morphazoidical

Shows the geometry data used for mapping. The workbench lists current form, contact, reader, and event values; the Feature Atlas defines the available fields.

13

Implementation

Morphazoid is a static site. It does not need a runtime server.

Interface
Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript modules. No UI framework.
Graphics
Canvas draws the instrument stages. A pixel budget caps resolution on large or high-density screens.
Audio
Web Audio handles synthesis, filters, routing, microphone input, decoding, and output. AudioWorklets handle continuous high-voice-count synthesis.
State
Pages load their default settings when opened. FM Drums can save edited banks in browser storage.
Development
Use npm run dev for the local server and npm run verify for checks and tests.
