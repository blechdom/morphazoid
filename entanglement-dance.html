Φ+ and Φ− are correlated states (both spin the same direction). Ψ+ and Ψ− are anti-correlated (they spin opposite). Decoherence untangles the partners.

Entanglement Dance exactly simulates a two-qubit Bell state and lets you hear the entanglement as a correlated square dance. Alice occupies the left channel and Bob the right. Press Space to play or pause the dance. Press C to call the next figure. Press M to collapse the state. Use Escape to turn audio off.
