Every chamber owns a blow reed and a draw reed. Signed pressure selects the primary tongue; the opposing tongue joins during bends while the moving vocal tract feeds a nonlinear acoustic load back into both reeds.

Hold the upper half of a numbered chamber to blow its upper reed, or hold the lower half to draw its lower reed. Move across the ten chambers while holding to glissando. Drag the HOLE / MOUTH node horizontally to select the anchor chamber and vertically to change the aperture. Pull either edge of the outlined mouth window in the control panel or either round edge of the canvas lip bracket to cover one through five holes directly. Use the Speed slider or drag AIR horizontally for logarithmic automatic breath speed and vertically for pressure. The Breath Shift slider ranges from a precise pressure reversal to a loose handoff with departing-reed ring, arriving-reed rise, and neighboring-chamber smear. Drag BEND vertically through hole-specific semitone stops and horizontally for reed gap. Drag the rhythm node horizontally for technique rate and vertically for amount. Drag TONGUE horizontally for tongue block and vertically for height, and drag CUP horizontally to sweep the cover-hand cavity resonance and vertically for growl. Drag LIPS inward or outward, and drag TRACT horizontally for throat opening and vertically for vocal feedback. The Breath rhythm selector drives patterned draw and blow attacks while Auto is on. Equivalent labeled controls follow the diagram. Number keys one through nine and zero select holes.
