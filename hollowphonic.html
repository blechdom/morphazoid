How it works / listening exercise

Try this first

Enable Audio and Play the noise source. Drag one chamber down to deepen it. Compare Wall bypass, then choose Strike the wall and tap a chamber.

What you hear

Noise or a drone passes through three resonating delay lines. The direct and resonated signals interfere. A strike excites those same chambers.

What the picture means

Chamber depth follows the resonator setting; moving lines show stored signal activity. Hz readouts describe nominal delay resonances, not detected pitches.

The mechanism

A deeper chamber has a longer delay and a lower nominal resonance. Wall damping removes stored energy. Coupling transfers energy around 1 → 2 → 3 → 1. This is a digital waveguide approximation, not a simulation of a particular building or material.

Controls

Cavity depth
Overall depth. Individual drags add an offset; deeper means lower resonance.
Wall damping
Higher damping shortens ringing and reduces high-frequency energy in the feedback.
Between chambers
Transfers feedback energy between chambers rather than keeping it within each chamber.
Through the wall
0% compares the source alone; 100% uses the resonated/interference output. Wall bypass overrides this mix.

Pause, reset, and memory

Play runs the continuous source. Pause stops that source but leaves decaying resonances and manual strikes available. Silent · strikes only needs taps, not Play. Microphone is silent until explicitly enabled. Reset restores depths and turns bypass off; no recordings are stored.

Audio Off is mute, not Pause. It closes owned microphone tracks but retains transport. Monitor can pass live microphone audio while paused until you turn it off.

Keyboard

Focus the stage. Left/right selects a chamber; Enter or 1–3 strikes it. Up/down changes depth. Space toggles the continuous source.

What this demo does not do

Three fixed resonators. No recorded loops, graph analysis, or conservation-budget display. Bypass compares the unfiltered source at the same output stage; it is not bit-perfect pass-through.

About this work-in-progress instrument

A coupled digital waveguide approximation, not a material simulation or an acoustic sum-rule demonstrator.

Presets change parameters, not capabilities. Reset controls keeps recordings and learned routes. Demo material is synthesized locally.
