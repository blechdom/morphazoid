What this means

open-map analogy

Classical trajectories can keep missing an opening on an increasingly dust-like trapped set. A finite wave cannot be confined to equally thin position and Fourier structures, so this toy open propagator sheds norm. One third is the triadic baker; off-default apertures use a grid-snapped three-block baker so its outer blocks exactly match the classical branches. This is an analogy for the role fractal uncertainty plays in open chaotic systems—not a proof and not a claim of uniform equidistribution.

Read the primary results: Bourgain–Dyatlov (2018) and Cohen (2025).

Escape Dust compares a seeded ensemble of classical phase-space points with an unnormalized finite wave state under a centered block baker map, triadic at the one-third default. The amber middle strip is removed at every step. Classical points that enter it are marked escaped. Wave amplitude in it is projected away before a matching finite unitary block-baker transform. No lost wave norm is restored.
