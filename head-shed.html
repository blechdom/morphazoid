How it works

The tape moves and the heads do not — the ring rotates past fixed heads, as on real hardware. Loop length divided by tape speed is the duration; speed also sets pitch.

Erase / hold is the one control that separates a delay from a looper. At 0 the tape is wiped behind the record head and you have an ordinary multi-tap delay. At 1 the erase is defeated and material accumulates — which is exactly what sound-on-sound mode did on an Echoplex.
