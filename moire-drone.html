Moiré Drone sends deterministic colored noise through one coupled 2D spectral fabric made from cyan warp and magenta weft filter banks. Its stretched gaps drive both resonant Q filters and direct FFT-bin attenuation: Q filtering supplies physical ringing edges, while FFT filtering can make exact, non-resonant spectral cuts. Tugs and controlled ring, lobe, spiral, or pulse ripples bend both filtering engines together. Gap stretch controls that coupling, Ripple stretch controls pitch displacement, and Pluck cut controls how deeply a pluck carves the noise. Tap the stage or press Enter to pluck. Drag vertically to tug a local patch. Press Space to freeze or resume spectral motion.
