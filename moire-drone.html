Fabric Filter sends colored, violet, crackle, stepped, fractal, or chaotic noise through one coupled 2D spectral fabric represented by a static pink-and-green vector grid. Pulling retains both coordinates of the grabbed patch: horizontal position selects frequency and warp location, while vertical position selects the weft row and character. Circular ripples, spherical-style lobes, spirals, pulses, gravity wells, stationary harmonic nodes, and side-to-side ocean fronts can interfere constructively or destructively before decaying.
