A pluck starts the modal lamella; signed airflow then preloads and sustains it. Exhalation bends the reed toward the outward gap and fills in brighter even partials. Inhalation reverses the load for a darker, cavity-weighted spectrum. Mouth articulation still selects which partial dominates without replacing the reed's fundamental.

Pull the copper reed trigger up or down and release it to pluck. Hold the Inhale or Exhale button to drive the vibrating reed manually, or leave Automatic breath cycle on for rhythmic bidirectional airflow. Drag anywhere in the BREATH pad in two dimensions: horizontal changes breath speed from one cycle per minute to twenty per second without restarting the current breath, and vertical changes pressure from zero through three hundred percent. Drag anywhere in the TEMPO pad horizontally from 36 to 480 BPM and vertically for alternating swing without restarting the breath pattern. Drag the tongue node in two dimensions, the jaw node vertically, and the lip node horizontally. Drag the focus and cavity node in two dimensions, or the glottis node horizontally, to reshape the resonator. Equivalent labeled controls are available in the control panel. Space or Enter plucks the reed. A, E, I, O, and U load mouth shapes and turn off vowel sequencing. Number keys one through five load harp bodies. R toggles the repeater. Left bracket holds inhale, right bracket holds exhale, and Escape stops it.
