A pluck starts the modal lamella; signed airflow then preloads and sustains it. Exhalation bends the reed toward the outward gap and fills in brighter even partials. Inhalation reverses the load for a darker, cavity-weighted spectrum. Mouth articulation still selects which partial dominates without replacing the reed's fundamental.

Pull the copper reed trigger up or down and release it to pluck. Hold the Inhale or Exhale button to drive the vibrating reed manually, or leave Automatic breath cycle on for rhythmic bidirectional airflow. Drag the tongue node in two dimensions, the jaw node vertically, the lip node horizontally, or the cavity node horizontally to change harmonic focus. Space or Enter plucks the reed. A, E, I, O, and U load mouth shapes. Number keys one through five load harp bodies. R toggles the repeater. Left bracket holds inhale, right bracket holds exhale, and Escape stops it.
